== find:war-conquest ==
War-conquest is three coupled layers. conquest.js runs the polity pass: hold capacity = CAP_K·instMul·log2(1+capPower/POW_REF) + seat bonuses, multiplied by a dominance tail (relative to the median capital), geography, imperial hysteresis, war/fiscal duress and momentum; provinces are budgeted cheapest-first, over-budget ones bleed loyalty and shed via tile-driven frontier secession, rebellion, governor ambition, or homeland restoration; fiscality (tax→treasury→army pay→debt→debasement→works) drives the insolvency death-spiral; peaceful absorption/engulfment/enclave-elimination consolidate the map. armies.js musters tier/food/manpower-capped garrisons, allocates one conserved national field army across fronts, grinds tiles/sieges with terrain, exhaustion and dyadic truces. cohesion.js era-weights identity forces off an emergent development pseudo-year. The system is genuinely emergent and causally rich, but the empire-size equilibrium is held by many overlapping stabilizers (some calibrated to the desired size distribution), and several correctness bugs lurk: a tier-constant regression that neuters town-scale rebellion, stale solvency/unrest stocks on single-member polities, and tick-window constants that break under SIM_GRANULARITY.

== find:demography-food ==
Demography-food is coherent and largely cardinal-rule-clean: population grows logistically toward K = food/perCapita (housing is bypassed under the default DISSOLVE_FARMS), food comes from territory fertility × a long chain of state-gated multipliers (agriGate, irrigation, alluvium, disease, aridity, soil fatigue, workability, livestock), grain flows up the liege tree for coin, and climate variability scales fertility globally. The CLAUDE.md calendar-anchor scar is genuinely fixed: ANCHOR_POP defaults to 0, and even when on, the target keys to the org-derived _civYear, not the clock. The Malthusian→industrial escape is emergent (_eraProd = f(agri knowledge, state org) plus the org+metallurgy-gated agronomy boost) — but only K escapes; growth rate never bends, so there is no demographic transition. Main defects: the unclamped logistic kills K-crashed cities instantly (even negative population), the farm-labour floor is charged on tiles the harvest sum excludes, fishing tech (fishFactor) is computed but never read, climate/soil passes ignore SIM_GRANULARITY, and the strictly coin-gated grain market makes pre-coinage cities impossible while its own header claims otherwise.

== find:money-economy ==
The currency arc runs as documented: mining from finite depositReserve is the sole default faucet (plus debasement seigniorage), COIN_LOSS_RATE the drain, and credit is genuinely default-OFF. I audited every mutation path — bilateral trade/tolls/tariffs/FX (sellGoods), luxury, the central-place grain market, taxes/rent/court/army-pay/debt/relief (disburseTreasury), tribute, colony endowments, pilgrimage, and the slave market — and each conserves coin arithmetically, with spare-above-reserve gates preventing negative wealth. slavery.js is fully wired (index.js pass, updateCoercedLabour, sack captives, mine/plantation coercion). Polity fiscal state is coherent: insolvency drives desertion, capacity throttling, war loans, then debasement. Real defects: the inflation baseline (_inflRef/_inflP) is not persisted, so save/load resets the whole price system; slave raiding double-scales with SIM_GRANULARITY; coin leaks to dead recipients (stale toll intermediates, unchecked slave sellers); rebellion loot is burned against the spec's no-burn constraint; the inflation baseline locks on a raw step>=5000 time gate (cardinal rule 1); and debasement mints proportional to the army bill rather than any coin stock (cardinal rule 2 tell).

== find:trade-networks ==
Trade-networks review (roads.js in full, transport.js, sea.js, plus callers in index.js). The freight burn-sink is confirmed gone: sellGoods conserves exactly — buyer's debit = seller's goods+carrier fee + intermediates' tolls/brokerage + state tariff (roads.js:1057-1112); the only supply regulator is COIN_LOSS_RATE. River-highway reach is present and correctly implemented (territory.js:314) and default OFF (T.RIVER_REACH def 0, tuning.js:111), matching commit 47c62bc. No cardinal-rule time gates found — all step%N uses are genuine cadences. Main findings: a critical save/load defect (_flowTiles never rebuilt from loaded roadFlow → experimentally verified 42× unbounded flow blowup, roads never abandon); the spice-quest guards read _seaReach right after updateSea nulls it (sated/already-reachable checks are dead → directed colonisation over-fires); river-corridor trade links are invisible to the component graph, so the planner builds self-defeating roads parallel to navigable rivers; plus toll leakage to dead intermediates, a linkMoney orientation error, and flow credited on zero-value trades. Design gaps: sea relay ports earn no entrepôt cut (the mechanism's own motivating case), and the road/river/sea per-tile cost ordering inverts historical bulk freight, patched by volume multipliers.

== find:territory-map ==
The territory-map subsystem runs every TERRITORY_INTERVAL (144) ticks: territory.js builds persistent per-settlement food catchments; countryTerritory.js builds the political map (per-settlement reach Voronoi → capital recolour → enclosed-waste/gap fills → smoothing → the new PR #38 mergePersistentTerritory → re-smoothing); countryClaim.js crawls the rendered border every 12 ticks and, via grownOwnerAt, feeds settlement allegiance back into the sim. The persistent merge matches the spec on land (worked land tracks the ledger, sticky marches, dead-owner cleanup — verified zero dead-country tiles right after a pass), but I confirmed six bugs empirically/synthetically: adoption trusts the stale rendered claim and resurrects dead countries (~1 zombie adoption per territory pass); the merge's land-only, core-seeded connectivity flood wipes 100% of across-water reach claims (mare nostrum dead by default); the catchment Dijkstra loses its claimant at water tiles so food catchments can never cross a strait; persist.js saves a nonexistent `_capturedAt` so conquest tile-holds are lost (and reverted) on load; post-merge smoothing recolours worked ledger land to rivals; and the reach ramp is unserialized. No cardinal-rule (time-gate/outcome-fit) violations found; the main design smell is six stacked corrective passes and a render layer that is secretly load-bearing.

== find:founding-state ==
The founding-state subsystem spans cradle seeding (state.js seedCradleVillage/seedEarthHearths), the crystallization sweep, settler parties and urban genesis (crystallize.js), and political adoption/founding (countryTerritory.js adoptAndFound/nucleateFrontierStates). Core mechanisms are genuinely emergent and unusually well-reasoned, but I confirmed the known anomaly: seedEarthHearths enforces no cross-hearth separation, and the 6-tile radius floor makes the Nile and Mesopotamia windows overlap on the smoke map — both cradles verifiably seat on tile (95,28). Second verified defect: crystallize/sendSettlers birth wilderness settlements INTO a realm, but adoptAndFound strips them stateless at the next territory pass until the border crawl re-adopts them (probe: 25/27 stripped, median 72 ticks after birth, ~288 ticks stateless, cut off from the realm food hierarchy). The coverage-tempo ramp (world.step / COVERAGE_RAMP, 'to full ~renaissance') is a direct cardinal-rule-1 time gate; d8b7df5's spacing calibration is half-mechanism but frozen against the wrong (and default-multiplied) spacing model; overseas independent genesis is dead code; floodplain oversampling and the DISSOLVE spacing constant are tuned to outcome shares.

== find:culture-faith ==
Culture, language and faith run as top-k per-settlement mixture layers updated on 150-tick, granularity-scaled passes; mixture normalization is sound (no NaN/negative paths found) and RNG substreams keep determinism. Four bugs: (1) branchLanguage seeds daughters from (parent.seed, parent.gen, step) only, so two forks of one parent in the same pass are byte-identical clone languages (verified by node repro) — sibling nations and multi-point drift then emit colliding names; (2) faiths' MIX_FLOOR pops every sub-5% increment each pass, making conversion an all-or-nothing cliff — folk faiths and weak/clashing state churches convert nobody ever, and low lever values silently freeze religion; (3) culture forks seedCulture whole settlements, wiping minority peoples and language state instantly; (4) schism/syncretism cooldowns aren't saved, so loaded worlds' religious pacing diverges. Design: several tile-distance constants and absolute per-pass world caps don't scale with map size (unlike the deliberately invariant cohesion radius); FAITH_SHAPE_RATE outpulls the intrinsic anchor 3:1, homogenizing state-faith personalities; colonies are reborn folk-pagan. No time-gate violations found — all timers are state-relative.

== find:dynasties ==
Dynasties samples royal genealogies per literate realm: governance form derived from state (verified emergent), succession by law with representation, crises on bare houses, legitimacy/trait levers read by wars, unrest and learning. Focus verdicts: (1) dynastic pacing does NOT read the cosmetic display calendar — it runs on `dynYear`, a uniform per-tick clock (a rate, not a gate); no cardinal-rule-1 time gate found. But that clock ignores SIM_GRANULARITY (`world._dt`), so dynastic/war-crisis pacing desyncs from all other history when the granularity lever moves, and stale comments still claim the layer runs on the display calendar. (2) Literacy gating is genuinely state-gated (first house appeared only when capital org crossed 0.22, ~step 6000 in my probe). (3) Person registry is a slow unbounded leak: 24k-step run gave persons 8694 and rising, dynasties map 780 (673 memberless, never deleted), plus two literally immortal off-roster royals (one aged 1850 dyn-years) from the MAX_HOUSE roster slice, and 172 cross-court couples double-breeding disjoint child sets. Cross-realm person entanglement (elections, marriages) breaks the one-ruler-per-realm assumptions in four places.

== find:events-history ==
The event log (events.js) is a sound design: structured append-only records, per-entity index, prose strictly render-time. Growth is bounded (measured 2567 events/15k steps at 360x180; EVENT_CAP=200000 safety valve) and I verified the true log is byte-identical after rendering every chronicle, perspective chronicle, and the full world-bible export — the biased views never mutate it. No cardinal-rule violations in these four files. The serious defects are in the polity lifecycle: reconcilePolities reconciles against a stale pre-pass countries map, so every same-pass secession is instantly logged "Dissolved" (102 ended vs 0 restored vs 0 emerged foundings in 15k steps; one polity has 11 ended events; 19 LIVING realms carry "Dissolved" in their chronicles), and silent ensurePolity calls re-open ended records without ever logging restoration, making reconcile's founded/restored branches provably dead. In historiography, coordinate-less TRAVELS events (era.reached, polity.ended) bypass the information horizon entirely (132/132 heard worldwide), the rumor-band clamp makes 35% of adjacent events mere rumor, three event types narrate as raw type strings, and compaction's id reassignment re-randomizes every tradition and freezes the worker's event ticker.

== find:tech ==
tech.js is a derived Civ-style tree: six continuous tracks (grown per-tick in settlement.js updateKnowledge under resource/climate gates) unlock ~78 techs via a fixed-point DAG pass; techEffects converts discovered techs (+0.6 partial credit for imminent ones) into blended bonus channels the sim reads (farmYield, military, reach, logistics, tradeMult...). Era = highest discovered tech era; I verified it is genuinely read-only — consumed only by chronicle events, the HUD, and the display calendar (calendar.js displayYear), never by a mechanic; dynasties run on a separate uniform clock. The cradle-distance gradient is honest (distance-decayed inheritance at crystallization + trade-graph diffusion; no cradle special-casing). Main problems: knowledge/ore/crop diffusion runs only over the ROAD graph while sea lanes carry trade and plague; several advertised tech outputs (defense, abilities, wealthMult) are dead code; Industrial/Modern health techs have zero mechanical expression; construction learning still keys off raw province population, defeating the documented urban-core fix; and an org→pseudo-year lookup detour survives as a fitted intermediary.

== find:core-loop ==
The core loop (index.js) is well-structured: a fixed pass order with documented one-tick lags (demographic anchor and _civYear read last tick's pop/countries; plague spreads on last tick's trade graph — all acceptable). RNG discipline is sound: no shared sequential stream, per-pass streams keyed on (seed,name,step) and per-entity on (seed,name,id), so adding a SYSTEM never perturbs another's dice; within-pass entity iteration order is deterministic. Calendar is cosmetic: stepToYear/displayYear feed only display; mechanics that need a "year" read world._civYear derived from leading-capital organisation (emergent), satisfying cardinal rule 1. Famine/plague are cadence-rolled but stochastic (not gated on a time point). Findings: (1) SIM_GRANULARITY fails to scale three cumulative-state clocks (climate walk, soil exhaustion, disease load), so G>1 silently alters the emergent history it promises to preserve; (2) WAR_DEF_SPLIT is a fully dead lever after the war-army conservation rewrite, with a stale comment; (3) ANCHOR_POP's description contradicts its def=0, leaving the historical-curve anchor mislabeled; (4) the money/pop conservation watch misses in-transit ships.

== find:persistence ==
persist.js saves identity (seed/meta/tuning) + dynamic state (settlements via a manual SETT_FIELDS whitelist, polities/cultures/faiths/persons/dynasties/events/ships wholesale, six typed-array maps, six Map tables), rebuilds terrain deterministically, and re-warms caches via computeTerritory. The smoke test's "save->load->hash identity" covers only step, a few settlement/polity numerics, events.length and a roadQuality sample — so a large class of state is lost silently. A headless probe (harness, seed 4242, 3000 steps) confirms: hash identical, yet load zeroes _tileCapturedAt (conquest holds), _soilFatigue (493 fatigued tiles), _orgApt/_riverAcc/_confine/_rugged on all 23 settlements, 4 live truces, war memory, inflation baseline, and reach ramps; 119/12800 territory tiles change ownership during loadWorld itself; original-continued vs loaded-continued runs diverge massively (+600 steps: pop 5405 vs 8045, 6 vs 7 countries). Version handling is reject-only (no migration). The recurring failure mode is the manual persistence whitelist drifting behind new mechanisms.

== find:worldgen-coupling ==
The worldgen->pipeline->peopleSim coupling is largely sound and genuinely single-source: buildTerritory (RES=1) runs once on the main thread and its outputs (tCrop, tFlood, deposits, rivers, tAncestry, tArrival) flow to the sim worker; tools use the same via _harness. The SPEC-climate-moisture-fix depletive-advection rewrite is implemented in moistureSolver.js (max-of-neighbours replaced by wind advection + a lossy diffuse term). One MAJOR bug: initRiverMag (state.js:268) point-samples the pixel-res riverMag into the sim's TILE_RES=2 grid, dropping ~64% of major/great river tiles (measured 35-36% retention) and fragmenting the navigable spine that territory reach, roads, confluences and transport all depend on — while fert/tFlood were correctly max-pooled. A latent coordinate mismatch (pipeline.js:303 indexes coastal by tile index) is harmless only at RES=1. The river moisture boost is also computed but discarded by the sim's moisture channel (only fert/tFlood carry it). Deposits/ancestry/wind point-sampling are acceptable (smooth/large features). Ideas: upwelling fisheries, deltas, loess.

== find:tools-validation ==
The tooling is genuinely deterministic (verified by double-run diff) and smoke is a solid CI gate, but validate does not measure what it advertises. Every history-shape gate is soft, so `npm run validate` can only fail on survival — the shape thresholds face no pressure and have drifted lax: Zipf accepts −0.5..−1.6 around a real-world −1, and under the default DISSOLVE model it ranks province (urban+rural) populations while claiming to test city sizes; the empire-tail ratio over member counts passes both near-uniform maps and total map-painters; lifespan bounds couple to argv STEPS and count restored-polity interregna as life; war gates are unnormalized and near-vacuous. Step-denominated gates also break under the SIM_GRANULARITY lever the harness explicitly supports. Parity: _harness/buildSim is app-identical, but earthRun.mjs hand-rolls init and drops tFlood/tAncestry — measuring a floodplain-less world the browser never simulates — and at gate resolutions the Nile and Mesopotamia hearths seed on the same tile. Several real stylized facts (population-growth shape, prices, trade share, war deadliness, culture scaling) are unmeasured.

== sweep:time-gates ==
Cardinal Rule 1 sweep across all of src/sim (worldgen + peopleSim, 40 files) and sim-adjacent src/*.js. Examined ~120 world.step reads and ~60 year/era-token hits. Classification: ~30 per-tick cadences (step % INTERVAL, correctly stretched by SIM_GRANULARITY), ~12 step-keyed caches, ~45 duration timers measuring emergent processes (conquest grace, plague/famine windows, truces, isolation-driven culture divergence, schism ages), ~25 bookkeeping timestamps (foundedStep etc.), ~6 RNG seedings, ~25 cosmetic/display uses (display calendar, dyn clock for human aging, chronicle, historiography, HUD era). The display calendar and era ribbon are genuinely read-only. Two true content-gates-on-time survive: the settlement-spread ramp (crystallize.js devFactor, COVERAGE_RAMP=17000 steps '~renaissance') and the inflation baseline lock (step>=5000, also granularity-blind). One scope flaw: the global leader's organisation drives identity salience (and anchored productivity) planet-wide, including uncontacted continents. Separately, save/load drops real cross-tick state — truces, _inflP/_inflRef, faith cooldowns — and leaves world.countries empty so _civYear reads -6000 for up to 150 post-load ticks.

== sweep:outcome-fitting ==
Reviewed the peopleSim economy/politics core (inflation, money, shocks, transport, countryTerritory, cohesion, agriculture, habitability, crystallize, state), worldgen cradle seeding, and persist. The engine is unusually disciplined about the cardinal rules: era gates read a development pseudo-year derived from the leading state's organization tech (civYearFromOrg), habitability/agriculture brakes are pure climate-state, cradle placement is an Earth-preset initial condition, and CAP_GEO keys on emergent geography. Named regions appear only in comments. Two real problems surfaced. (1) A genuine time gate and an associated save/load fidelity bug live in the inflation baseline: it locks on world.step >= 5000 and is not persisted, so reloading a mature world re-bases prices and permanently diverges the economy. (2) Identity-era salience (nationalism/religion) is driven by a single GLOBAL leading-org clock and applied to every realm, so ancient societies inherit modern nationalist forces the instant any civ industrializes. The demographic anchor still fits world population to a hardcoded curve (documented, lever-gated). Bugs ranked below.

== sweep:determinism ==
Determinism architecture is fundamentally sound: no Math.random/Date.now in src/sim (performance.now is profiling-only), rng is cleanly partitioned into pass/entity streams (rng.js), rng-consuming loops iterate id-sorted snapshots (faiths.js) or hash per-entity (conquest, historiography), sorts are numeric+stable, id counters are serialized, and worker vs main-thread generation is identical for all worker-eligible presets. Smoke passes; two same-seed sims match. The failures cluster in SAVE/LOAD: persist.js's schema has drifted from the sim. Verified empirically (320×160, seed 4242): save at step 3000, continue 1440 steps both ways → uninterrupted pop 6689 / 25.2% land vs loaded 11019 / 30.9% — a systematic bias from lost cross-tick state: soil fatigue (critical), truces, the capture clock (persist saves a dead field _capturedAt instead of _tileCapturedAt), reach ramps, and an empty countries view driving _civYear to -6000 post-load. Separately: real-wind/import saves rebuild wrong terrain; Earth hearths stack on one tile at test resolutions; sea-lane wobble is seeded from a nonexistent field, identical across all seeds.

== sweep:conservation ==
Conservation audit of src/sim/peopleSim/. Money: every trade path checked (bilateral goods+freight+tolls+brokerage+tariff in roads.sellGoods, luxury legs, food-hierarchy coin-down, pilgrimage, slave market, construction supplier payments, taxes/rent/court/army-pay/debt/relief in conquest, colony endowments) sums exactly — the closed supply holds in-run, with intended faucets (mining+seigniorage, debasement, credit) and drains (COIN_LOSS_RATE, dry mines). The genuine leaks are at the edges: save/load drops `_credit` (re-mints credit), `_inflRef/_inflP` (price level rebases), `_tileCapturedAt` (saved under a dead name), and `_truces`; dying settlements destroy their hoards; ravage 'loots' coin into nothing; extreme spend-levers can drive treasuries negative. Population: births/famine are the designed faucet/drain; migration, settlers, sea colonies, captives→unfree, slave market all conserve — but ships/captives/unfree sit outside the invariant watch, and slave raiding double-scales with SIM_GRANULARITY. Deposits deplete correctly. Registries are bounded (events compacted, persons pruned, roads decay; polities persist by design). Cardinal-rule items: inflation's step≥5000 gate, and the default-on hard-coded Earth hearths (which also co-locate two cradles on one tile — seen in the smoke log).

== sweep:save-load ==
Cross-cutting save/load sweep: diffed every field assigned in src/sim/peopleSim/*.js against persist.js. RNG needs no serialization (streams are stateless, re-derived from seed+step/id; counters ARE saved) — that part is sound. But the whitelist has real holes: the save writes a dead field (_capturedAt) while the live conquest-hold map (_tileCapturedAt, Float64) is dropped; per-country reach ramps (_cBudgetRamp) reset every loaded empire to cradle reach; soil exhaustion (_soilFatigue), truces, inflation baseline/price maps (_inflRef/_inflP), heritable aptitude (_orgApt), banking credit (_credit) and several throttles all vanish on load; and creation-only settlement statics (_riverAcc/_confine/_rugged) are neither saved nor recomputed, turning loaded river cradles into penalized desert. Restore order: loadWorld never rebuilds world.countries (empty for up to 150 ticks — dynasties, faith-state, muster, _civYear degrade) and warms territory before the climate overlay. The smoke hash covers none of this, so the roundtrip test passes while loaded worlds silently diverge. Bonus: a stale world.personalities read leaves the expansionism claim multiplier dead.

== sweep:performance ==
peopleSim runs one monolithic tick loop (index.js) with interval-amortized passes. Measured on the 480x240 sim grid (EARTH_W=960, seed 8817): avg tick grows 1.6ms @23 settlements to 16.2ms @238 (24k steps); worst single ticks grow 174ms to 1197ms, recurring on multiples of 600 where sea+polity+culture+faith+war+muster+trade+chronicle all fire together. CPU profile (16k steps): per-settlement-per-tick economics dominate (updateFood 8.7%, updateKnowledge 6.6%, coerced labour 2.6%, luxury 2.0%, population 2.0% — ~27% combined); the trade pass grows super-linearly because ports keep 64 sea peers against the documented 12-partner land cap (16ms/tick @238); updatePolities is a ~0.7s monolith per pass; relaxClaim burns 2.8% rescanning all N tiles per ring. The shipped browser grid is 4x these tile counts, so 50k-step runs with hundreds of settlements will sustain >50ms ticks and multi-second hitches. Spatial-grid adoption is thorough, the event log is bounded/indexed, no per-tick scans grow with history length. One cardinal-rule violation found: crystallize's coverage ramp gates settlement spread on world.step.

== sweep:integration-deadcode ==
Integration verdict: the load-bearing loops are real, not cosmetic. Money constrains war end-to-end (treasury -> wage bill -> war loans -> debasement -> solvency -> garrison desertion + capacity throttle). Tech changes combat via the military channel (but the defense/fish/wealth channels and all five ability flags are dead). Culture drives loyalty/secession/absorption/war-targeting through cohesion.js, era-weighted off emergent development. Faith reaches loyalty, casus belli, pilgrimage coin, schisms, and even shapes state personality. slavery.js is fully wired (raids + war captives -> market -> _unfree -> mining/cash-crops/revolts, persisted). Climate feeds agriculture via the territory tally — but volcanic winters decay faster than that tally samples, so the headline shock is a no-op. Shocks feed unrest and the event log. Personality moves real levers (attack bar, army size, roads, reach, colonies). Dead weight found: one unread tuning lever (WAR_DEF_SPLIT) of 213, OUT_MILITARY, localPByCountry. One raw time gate survives (inflation step>=5000); Earth hearths hard-code named outcomes; persistence drops several cross-tick states.

== run:long-run ==
Empirical long-run hunt: 480x240 earth_sim seed 4242 for 30000 steps (250s), seeds 777/31337 for 10000 each, invariants ON throughout — zero invariantHits, no NaN/Infinity anywhere, no extinction, no frozen dynamics. Population follows a healthy S-curve (75 → 611k) as per-settlement _eraProd rises with agriculture; coin supply equilibrates ~3.4–3.6M under the mine-faucet/coin-loss drain; eras advance Stone→Modern purely off knowledge (civYear 1862 at step 30000); countries churn 19–27 while persistent registries stay bounded (persons pruned back to ~9–13k, events 7.9k vs 200k cap; cultures/polities/dynasties grow slowly by design). Two genuine anomalies surfaced: (1) the HUD/history totalWealth sawtooths −53% every POLITY_INTERVAL because peopleSimStats sums stale c._treasury snapshots while live treasuries absorb ~70% of civilian coin mid-window (probe: gap 9k→290k of ~490k, oscillating at exactly step%150); (2) the mobility knowledge track is dead content — max 0.312 at leadingEra 6 (chariots need 0.45, cavalry 0.70), so the entire horse/steppe branch never fires on any seed. Plus one cardinal-rule time gate (inflation baseline locks at step 5000) and a wrong-side load comparison in the virgin-soil contact sweep.

== run:cradle-probe ==
Cradle seeding lives in src/sim/peopleSim/state.js. Two paths: an algorithmic scorer (fertility + river + circumscription, CRADLE_MIN_SEP=60 tiles, up to 10 cradles) and — default-ON for earth presets — seedEarthHearths: three hand-placed real-Earth hearths (Nile, Mesopotamia, Yellow River) at fixed fractional coordinates, each snapped to the best river tile inside a per-site search window. Empirical probe (harness-identical builds): at the smoke config (320x160, seed 4242, earth_sim) the Nile and Mesopotamia hearths seat on the IDENTICAL tile (95,28) — collision CONFIRMED. The two windows overlap structurally at every size (measured 5-8 tiles at tw=160/240/480) and the fixed-hearth path has no dedup or separation check. Downstream, the duplicate "Mesopotamia" holds 0 territory tiles through t=3000 and starts inside the Nile's polity — the Fertile Crescent civilization never exists, and CI passes anyway. At 480x240 (seeds 8817, 4242) and 960x480 all three seats are distinct and valid (riverMag>=2, fert>=0.82, floodplain on the river cradles). The hand-seated coordinates with drift-fitted radii violate cardinal rule 2 and are the path both CI gates measure.

== run:history-shape ==
Empirical 30k-step run (480x240, seed 8817, 2x validate length): population 5.9k→2.25M sim-units, tracking a plausible historical curve with the anchor OFF (fully emergent); settlements 36→103, countries 5→31. Tech follows a clean S-curve but saturates — construction 0.99 by step 15000, org/metallurgy 0.99 by 27000, civYear pinned at 1960 — while mobility never exceeds 0.32: cavalry is never invented anywhere. War escalates ~80× to 333 new wars/1000 steps (permanent pair-saturated war). Hegemony ossifies: the Yellow-River cradle holds #1 for 74% of samples (unbroken steps 13800→28500) and polity mortality collapses 7× late. The validate lifespan warn (max/median 2.4) is diagnosed: the fallen sample is a censored churn layer — the true heavy tail is ALIVE (two cradles at age 30000; ratio rises to 6.8 at 30k) — and lifecycle records are corrupted by two bugs: reconcilePolities kills every secession-born polity at birth against a stale country view (70 ended events vs 28 recorded openings at 15k; median measurable life 0 steps), and govOf silently resurrects dead records.
