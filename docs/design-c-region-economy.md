2. THE LABEL INVARIANT AND THE EXTRACTION OPERATOR (T.LABEL_SETT, def 0 until validated)
========================================================================
Ruling (extends the urban floor): a settlement entity exists exactly where the population field sustains a town-scale concentration; the entity is the NAME/story/economy of that concentration, never its physical cause. Extraction is a pure deterministic READ (no RNG — unlike the sweep it replaces); it only ever BIRTHS; death stays demographic (see §4).

Two channels, both field-read:

(A) URBAN labels — the concentration read (the Tier-C endpoint). u[ti] = max(0, pf[ti] − baseCap[ti]) where baseCap = capField minus the stamped urban spikes (world._urbanSpike, popField.js:1459,:1627; applyUrbanSpikes :1246 — subtract e.k shares to recover the terrain-only capacity). Physical meaning: URBAN population = people the ground under them does not feed (exactly deriveOnePop's kBeyond concept, popField.js:1557-1559, applied tile-wise). Every maximal connected component of u>0 with Σu ≥ U_MIN is one label; seat = argmax u, site-score tiebreak (§3). A metropolis+suburbs component is ONE label (size, not count — tiers are already percentile-ranked post-B3). Matching for identity: a component claims every existing settled entity whose home tile lies inside it (or within the urban footprint radius, urbanCoreR popField.js:1216); a component with no entity births one; components never delete or merge entities. U_MIN is frozen at activation as the largest value under which extraction reproduces the live entity set exactly (bijection), then persisted — a unit-calibration freeze in the _onePopScale/POW_FIELD median-anchor tradition (popField.js:1470-1486), not a supply dial. NOTE the honest dependency: today concentrations exist only at entity cores (urbanConcentrate is entity-anchored, popField.js:1337,:1585-1586), so channel A alone reproduces ~90 — its supply GROWS when the physical half of Tier C (agglomeration acting at field peaks instead of entity cores — the companion lane) lands. Extraction is deliberately ready for that with zero further changes: it reads structure, wherever structure comes to exist.

(B) FRONTIER/RURAL labels — the countryside read (replaces the sweep, and delivers scaling NOW). Operator = the probe's greedy packing, existing-labels-first: (1) every existing label claims its basin (nearest tiles within LABEL_REACH, up to its own catchment mass); (2) residual peaks (pf argmax over unclaimed land, tie → lower ti) gather unclaimed mass nearest-first within LABEL_REACH; a peak that accumulates ≥ BASIN_BAR births a rural market-town label there, claiming those tiles for the pass; repeat until no peak clears the bar; integrate at most LABEL_RATE births per firing (a pace constant — the NUCLEATE_MAX_PASS=4 anti-bloom / EXPAND_RATE precedent: towns take years to build; controls approach speed, never the equilibrium — verify insensitivity by sweep). Separation floor = the urban footprint only (twin towns allowed; sub-grid geometry cannot bind — §0). BASIN_BAR = TOWN_FOUND_MIN / world._onePopScale — ZERO new constants: "the field mass whose ONE_POP-derived census is a viable town seed (90)" — self-calibrating, unit-stable (the drift fix for TOWN_BASIN_MIN), ≈70k field-people at the measured scale → supply ~115-213 at 12k (bracketed by the 20k-bar curve), growing with density, vs 78 entities. With the census-viability floor variant (URBAN_SEED_MIN=25 → ≈20k field) supply is 213 at 12k (2.7×). The bar's census-side floor is what keeps every birth clear of the wither machinery (settlement.js:3208 — census <8 for 2000 ticks dies): birth census ≥ 90 ≫ 8 gives a built-in ~11× birth/death hysteresis — no flicker by construction.

Birth mechanics (channel B; channel A births identically at the blob peak): makeSettlement (settlement.js:282) with people = claimed basin mass × _onePopScale; NO fieldShift debit (crystallize.js:746 retires) and SKIP makeSettlement's ONE_POP credit (settlement.js:420) for label births — a label is a read; its people already stand on the ground; founding stops moving anyone (concentration into the core is urbanConcentrate's job). Σpeople conservation becomes exact by omission — smoke-checked. Knowledge: the existing donor inheritance verbatim (inheritKnowledgeAt :1451 + NATION_TECH_FLOOR :216), plus floor at the local devField technique (popField.js:84-166) so a town born of a developed countryside is born developed. Culture/independent-origin logic verbatim (crystallize.js:596-618 — keyed on transportDist, unchanged; keep the transport refresh :339-342). Politics at birth verbatim: grownLiveOwnerAt (:595), statecraft symmetry (:630-659), fiscAdoptable (:728-731), stateless otherwise — only the DICE are replaced by the READ.

Cadence: extraction fires in the territory block (index.js:155-161), immediately after nucleateFrontierStates — it needs last pass's _territoryOwner/_countryOwner (the CATCHMENT_CLIP 1-pass-stale precedent), and the next territory pass carves the newborn's catchment (deriveOnePop already tolerates the catchment lag, popField.js:1601). Cost: one O(N) component scan + bounded ring walks per firing, at the already rNorm-stretched TERRITORY_INTERVAL — measured 25-98ms at tw=240 in the probe, amortized fine.

## Risks

1. PERF (the big one): labels 2-4× at 12k and growing with density; per-settlement passes are ~70% of the tick at ~90 entities (field-polity-spec §perf), so naive 4× entities ≈ 3× tick cost, worse late and at 960. Mitigations to design with the implementation: tiered update cadence for small rural labels, aggregation of sub-town economics, choosing the BASIN_BAR operating point; the default-flip must be gated on the measured perf curve (validation §7). 2. HOSTILITY GAP (hard dependency): capField has NO disease/hostility term (verified popFieldKernel.js:26-72 — fert·dev, water access, relief only); today the malaria/arid thinning of TOWNS lives in the retired spacing multiplier (crystallize.js:138-151). Retiring the sweep without first moving settleHostility into the field (a capacity/mortality mechanism — disease kills people, not permits; the rule-2-correct home) re-densifies the wet tropics and undoes Tier-A #5 work. Sequence it BEFORE or WITH this lane. 3. REGIME SHIFT on entity-count-quantized consumers: roads (kin radius), muster/garrison distributions (B4c's P85 fortress bars re-derive), stylized measurement constants (Zipf 50-urban floor, clustering, fallen-lifespan) — the leaner-world-reckoning recalibration discipline applies (documented re-derivation, never quiet). 4. CHANNEL-A SUPPLY IS PHYSICS-DEPENDENT: until the companion lane re-anchors agglomeration to field peaks, concentration-read supply reproduces ~90; the scaling this lane delivers NOW comes from channel B (frontier/rural). If the physical half stalls, the design still stands but the urban hierarchy stays entity-seeded. 5. CATCH-UP WAVE on old saves / lever flip: bounded by LABEL_RATE but still a visible settling era; chronicle/event volume and realm-adoption churn need watching in the windowed A/B. 6. CROSS-GRID FIELD NON-INVARIANCE (measured 4.17× at equal step, tw=120 vs 240): all bars go through per-world frozen bridges so labels inherit no absolute cross-grid constant, but the underlying field-invariance gap belongs on the resolution-invariance plan's ledger. 7. Component-matching edge cases (blob split/merge over a shrinking city): mitigated by births-only extraction + footprint matching, but the merge case (two entities in one blob) must be explicitly allowed to persist as two labels, or cities sharing a conurbation would flicker.
