# Heraldry symbol library — a vocabulary for the armorial grammar

A working catalogue of charges, devices, and motifs for `src/sim/heraldry.js` to draw
from. It is deliberately **vast** — the point is a wide space to choose from — but it is
organised around one rule that keeps the range from collapsing into incoherent pastiche:

> **Two tiers. A UNIVERSAL pool the grammar may generate freely for any house; and
> CONTEXTUAL motifs that appear only when the world's emergent state warrants them.**

This mirrors the project's cardinal rules and the design we already settled on: most of a
coat is an abstract visual language (lions, chevrons, mullets — meaningless in themselves,
meaningful by lineage), while the *loaded* symbols — a dharmachakra, a Maasai shield, a
sun-disc — must be earned by what a realm has become (its faith's character, its biome, its
subsistence, its materials), never stamped on at random and never keyed to a named
real-world civilisation. Build the family; let the emergent state pick from it.

Nothing here is a spec to hard-code case by case — it is the *vocabulary*. The generator
already assigns meaning through genealogy, faith-tincture, and canting; new entries extend
the pool the generator samples, with the contextual ones behind an emergent gate.

## Part I — The universal pool (generate freely for any house)

These are abstract or near-universal. Any house may bear them; a stock's *tradition*
(the family aesthetic seed) biases which categories it favours, exactly as now.

### Beasts (mammals) — take an ATTITUDE
lion, lioness, tiger, leopard/panther (heraldic "pantheon" incuensed), bear, boar, wolf,
fox, stag/hart, hind, roebuck, bull/ox, cow, horse, ass/mule, ram, sheep/lamb (Paschal
lamb = Agnus Dei), goat, he-goat, dog (talbot, greyhound, mastiff, alaunt), hare/coney
(rabbit), squirrel, hedgehog/urcheon, otter, beaver, elephant, camel, dromedary, ape/monkey,
domestic cat, wildcat, marten, ermine/weasel, badger/brock, mole, ox, buffalo, bison,
antelope (heraldic), reindeer, moose/elk.

**Attitudes** (a beast's pose — a whole extra axis of variety):
rampant (rearing), passant (walking), statant (standing), salient (leaping), sejant
(sitting), sejant erect, couchant (lying, head up), dormant (sleeping), courant (running),
combatant (two rampant facing), addorsed (back to back), guardant (face to viewer),
reguardant (looking back). Heads alone: caboshed/cabossed (front, no neck), couped
(cut straight), erased (torn). Parts: gamb/jamb (leg), paw, head, "attired" (antlers),
"armed & langued" (claws/tongue a different tincture).

### Birds — often "displayed" or "close"
eagle (displayed; **double-headed eagle** = imperial/dual claim), falcon, hawk, kite,
raven/crow, cornish chough, dove (often with olive sprig), swan, pelican ("in her piety",
vulning her breast), martlet (footless swallow — the classic small bird), cock/rooster,
peacock ("in his pride", tail displayed), owl, crane ("in its vigilance", holding a stone),
stork, heron, ostrich (often holding a horseshoe), popinjay/parrot, swallow, lark,
kingfisher/halcyon, moorcock, curlew, pheasant, dove, goose, duck/shoveler, eaglet.

### Fish & sea creatures — often "naiant" (swimming) or "hauriant" (upright)
dolphin (the heraldic dolphin, bowed), luce/pike, salmon, trout, barbel, herring, ged,
chabot, whale, whelk, **escallop** (scallop shell — a top-tier charge, pilgrim's badge),
crab, lobster, crayfish/écrevisse, eel, sea-star, cockle, mussel.

### Reptiles, amphibians, insects
serpent/snake (nowed = knotted; involved; glissant), adder, lizard, salamander (in flames),
toad/frog, tortoise, bee ("industry" — a favourite), ant/emmet, grasshopper, butterfly,
fly, spider, scorpion, stag-beetle, gadfly, cricket, hornet.

### Monsters & mythical — the deep well of fantasy heraldry
dragon (4 legs, bat wings), **wyvern** (2 legs, winged, barbed tail), **cockatrice/basilisk**
(cock-headed wyvern, "kills by gaze"), **griffin/gryphon** (eagle fore + lion hind; male
griffin/keythong = rayed, wingless), hippogriff, **unicorn**, **pegasus**, hydra (many-headed),
chimera, harpy (bird-woman), **mermaid/melusine** (often with mirror & comb), merman/triton,
siren, sphinx, **phoenix** (rising from flames), salamander, sea-horse (morse), sea-lion,
sea-dog, sea-wolf, yale/centicore (swivelling horns), enfield (fox-eagle-wolf), opinicus,
allocamelus, camelopard (heraldic giraffe), camelopardel, **manticore/man-tiger**, satyr,
**centaur/sagittary** (archer-centaur), tyger (heraldic — not the natural tiger), calopus,
bagwyn, **wodewose/wild man** (club-bearing savage), lindworm, amphisbaena (two-headed
serpent), basilisk, winged bull/lion (lamassu-like), angel, cherub, seraph.

### Human figure & body parts
king/queen crowned, knight in armour, **savage/wild man**, moor's/blackamoor's head,
saracen's head, maiden/woman, bishop, monk, **arm** (embowed; armoured "in armour";
holding a weapon — a "cubit arm"), **hand** (dexter/sinister; the **Red Hand** of Ulster/O'Neill),
gauntlet, **heart** (flaming heart; crowned heart), leg/limb, skull & bones (mortality),
eye, three legs conjoined (triskelion — Isle of Man/Sicily), foot.

### Plants
**rose** (heraldic 5-petal, barbed & seeded; Tudor double rose), **lily / fleur-de-lis**
(and the natural lily), **thistle** (Scotland), **shamrock/trefoil** (Ireland), clover,
oak (tree, leaves, **acorns**), laurel (branch, wreath), **olive branch** (peace), palm
(tree, frond — victory), holly, ivy, mistletoe, **vine** (leaf, grapes, bunch), **garb**
(wheat sheaf), wheat-ear, barley, rye, maize/corn cob, columbine, **cinquefoil**, **trefoil**,
**quatrefoil**, sunflower, marigold, gillyflower/carnation, **lotus**, **pomegranate**
(Granada), pineapple, apple, pear, quince, strawberry & leaf, teasel, bulrush/reed,
fern, bluebell, primrose, tree "eradicated" (roots shown) vs "couped", tree-stump/stock,
slip (cut branch), leaf (single), broom-plant (planta genista → Plantagenet).

### Celestial & natural features
**sun in splendour** (rayed disc, often a face; straight+wavy rays = light+heat), sun
demi/issuing, rays/estoile-burst, **crescent** (increscent = horns to dexter, decrescent =
to sinister, "in her complement" = full moon), moon with face, **mullet** (5-point star;
6/7/8-pointed variants; **pierced mullet** = spur-rowel), **estoile** (6 wavy points),
**star cluster / constellation** (e.g. a cross of stars — see Southern Cross below), comet
(blazing star), cloud, **rainbow**, **thunderbolt** (Jove's — winged flames & darts),
lightning flash, **flames / fire** (a "fire-beacon"), **water** (barry-wavy = sea/river; a
single **bar wavy** = a river; **fountain** = roundel barry-wavy blue-white), **fountain/well**,
**mount / mountain** (a "trimount" — three green hills), **rock**, island, wave, drops
(gouttes — of blood/water/gold), whirlpool (gurges), star-of-life-many-rays.

### Weapons & armour
**sword** (also seax/falchion, scimitar), **dagger/poniard**, **spear/lance**, pike,
javelin, **halberd/poleaxe/battle-axe**, **mace**, war-hammer, **bow** (& **arrow**;
**pheon** = barbed broad arrow-head, points down; broad-arrow), **crossbow**, quiver,
**caltrap** (4-spiked horse-trap), **helmet / helm** (great-helm, morion, barrel-helm),
**shield / escutcheon** (as a charge), **buckler**, breastplate, **spur** & spur-rowel,
stirrup, **banner / pennon / gonfalon**, **crossed swords / crossed spears** (a very
common martial arrangement), sheaf of arrows (bound), bomb/grenade ("fireball"),
cannon, **war-horn**.

### Tools, trades & mechanisms
**hammer**, **anvil**, **axe** (broadaxe, adze), **sickle**, **scythe**, **plough/coulter**,
**spade**, **pickaxe / miner's pick**, saw, **shears / scissors**, pincers/tongs, **wheel**
(cartwheel; **Catherine wheel** = spiked/torturous; **mill-rind / fer-de-moline** = the iron
of a millstone), **gear / cogwheel** (industry — modern), **cog & hammer** (labour), **key**
(often crossed keys — St Peter), **buckle** (fermail), **horseshoe**, **chain**, **fetterlock/
padlock**, **water-bouget** (twin water-skins on a yoke), **maunch** (a dangling medieval
sleeve — pure heraldic oddity), distaff, **spindle / shuttle** (weaving), comb (**Duafe** in
Adinkra too), net, hook, **anchor** (& foul anchor), **rudder / helm (ship's wheel)**, oar,
**scales / balance** (justice), **hourglass** (time/mortality), **quill / pen**, **book**
(open = learning, closed, clasped), **scroll**, **inkpot**, plumb-bob, level, trowel,
compass & square (masonry), **lamp / lantern** (learning), torch/firebrand, taper/candle,
**bell**, **hunting-horn / bugle** (stringed), **harp** (Ireland), trumpet, **clarion / rest**
(an enigmatic wind-instrument charge), lyre, drum.

### Everyday objects, regalia & structures
**crown / coronet** (mural, naval, celestial, eastern/antique), **mitre**, **cap of
maintenance**, cardinal's hat, **Phrygian / liberty cap** (on a pole — republican freedom),
gauntlet, glove, boot, purse/pouch, **goblet / cup / chalice / ciborium**, ewer, platter,
pitcher, **ring / annulet**, brooch, **chess-rook**, dice, cushion/pillow (tasselled),
**letter of the alphabet**, **tower**, **castle** (two towers + embattled wall + gate),
**portcullis**, **gate / arch / bridge**, **fortress / citadel**, **beehive** (skep — industry),
**ship / lymphad** (single-masted galley, oars in action), **galley / longship**, boat,
**caravel**, wheel-of-fortune, **pyramid**, column/pillar, obelisk, temple/rotunda,
lighthouse/pharos.

### Crosses — a family of their own (dozens of forms)
Latin cross, Greek cross, **cross patté / formée** (arms flaring), cross pattée-fitchée
(pointed foot — to plant in the ground), **cross flory / fleury** (fleur-de-lis ends),
**cross moline** (forked, mill-rind ends), cross patonce, **cross bottony / trefly**
(trefoil ends), **cross crosslet** (each arm crossed — often **semé/crusily**), **cross
potent** (T-ends → **Jerusalem cross** = potent + 4 crosslets, "crusader"), **Maltese cross**
(eight-pointed), **St George's cross** (plain, red on white), **saltire** (St Andrew's; St
Patrick's = red saltire), **Tau cross** (T), **cross calvary** (on steps/degrees), **Celtic /
ringed cross**, **cross of Lorraine** (double bar), **Papal cross** (triple bar), **Russian
Orthodox cross** (three bars, slanted foot), **Coptic cross**, cross raguly (branch-cut),
cross fourchée (forked), cross pommée (round ends), cross avellane (hazelnut ends),
cross gringolée (serpent ends), Chi-Rho, Jerusalem cross, ankh (Egyptian — see Part II).
*(The fylfot/gammadion appears historically but is excluded — modern connotation.)*

### Sub-ordinaries & pure-geometric charges
**roundel** (tinctured names: bezant=gold, plate=silver, torteau=red, hurt=blue, pomme=green,
golpe=purple, pellet/ogress=black, **fountain**=barry-wavy), **annulet** (ring; interlaced
annulets), **billet** (upright rectangle; **billetty** = strewn), **lozenge** (diamond),
**fusil** (narrow lozenge), **mascle** (voided lozenge), **rustre** (round-pierced lozenge),
**fret** (interlaced — the "Harington knot"), **fretty** (lattice), gyron, gore, **orle**
(inner border), **tressure** (double tressure flory-counter-flory = Scotland), **label**
(cadency — eldest son), **inescutcheon** (shield-on-shield — pretension), flaunches, **pile**,
pall/pairle, **chevronel**, **bar / barrulet**, gemel (paired bars), **lozenge-throughout**,
triangle, delf (square), goutte (drop).

### Field treatments (multiply everything above)
- **Partitions:** per pale, per fess, per bend (& sinister), per chevron, per saltire,
  quarterly, **per cross**, **gyronny** (8/12), per pall, **chequy** (chequerboard),
  **lozengy / fusily**, **barry / paly / bendy / chevronny** (N stripes), barry-bendy,
  paly-bendy, **pily**, quarterly-of-nine, **counterchanged** (tinctures swap across the line).
- **Partition & ordinary lines:** straight, **engrailed** (scallops out), **invected**
  (scallops in), **embattled** (crenellated), **indented** (sawtooth), **dancetty** (deep
  zigzag), **wavy/undy**, **nebuly** (cloud-lobed), **raguly** (lopped branch), **dovetailed**,
  **potenty** (T-shaped), urdy (pointed).
- **Furs** (a whole alternate "tincture" class): **ermine** (white, black tails), ermines
  (black, white tails), erminois (gold), pean (gold-on-black), **vair** (blue-white bells),
  counter-vair, potent, counter-potent.
- **Semé** (powderings — strew any small charge): **semé-de-lis** (fleurs), **crusily**
  (crosslets), **bezanty / platy** (roundels), **goutty** (drops — of blood/water/gold),
  **estoilly / mullety** (stars), **billetty** (billets), semé of hearts, semé of bees.

## Part II — Contextual / world motifs (gate to emergent state)

These carry strong cultural meaning. Fold them into the pool **only behind an emergent gate**
— a faith's doctrinal character, a biome, a subsistence mode, available materials, a
governing style — expressed as *families*, never as "this realm is Japan." A realm whose
emergent faith is contemplative-cosmological might draw a wheel/lotus/knot family; a
pastoral-warrior steppe culture a horse/shield/spear family; a maritime island culture a
canoe/shell/frigatebird family. The generator picks within the family; the world picks the
family.

### East Asian family (contemplative / imperial / cyclical)
Eastern **dragon** (long, four/five-clawed — imperial), **fenghuang** (Chinese phoenix),
**taijitu** (yin-yang), **bagua trigrams** (the eight three-line signs; four-of-eight as on
the Korean **taegeuk** flag), **tomoe** (comma-swirl, 1–3), **mon / kamon** motifs
(chrysanthemum, paulownia/kiri, hollyhock/aoi, cherry-blossom, bamboo, wisteria, crane-roundel),
**rising-sun disc** (plain) and **rayed sun** (16-ray), **crane & tortoise** (longevity),
koi/carp, pine, plum-blossom.

### South & Southeast Asian family (dharmic / monsoon / temple)
**dharmachakra** (8- or 24-spoke wheel), **lotus** (padma), **Ashoka lion-capital** (4 lions
addorsed), **stupa**, **Angkor-type temple** silhouette, **Garuda** (eagle-man mount),
**naga** (hooded serpent, often multi-headed), **white elephant** (royal), **Khanda** (Sikh —
double-edged sword + chakkar + two kirpans), **Om**, **trishula** (trident), **kris/keris**
(wavy dagger), **hamsa** goose, bodhi/pipal leaf, betel, areca palm, peacock (Indian).

### Middle Eastern / Islamic / Persian family (aniconic / solar-lunar / martial)
**crescent & star** (the dominant pairing), **star alone** (5/6-point — **Seal of Solomon**
= interlaced hexagram, historically on Morocco/Nigeria), **crossed scimitars / two swords**
(Saudi-type), **Zulfiqar** (bifurcated sword of Ali), **Omani khanjar** (curved dagger over
crossed swords), **Eagle of Saladin**, **Hawk of Quraysh**, **palm tree** (& palm + crossed
swords), **cedar** (Lebanon — holiness/eternity), **date palm**, mosque/dome & **minaret**,
lamp/lantern, **Faravahar** (Zoroastrian winged disc), **Lion & Sun** (old Persia — solar
lion), **Roj** sun (Kurdish, 21 rays), calligraphic band (as an abstract inscription bar).

### African family (wildlife / ancestral / independence)
**Maasai/Nguni shield + crossed spears** (pastoral-warrior), **knobkerrie / assegai**,
**Great-Zimbabwe soapstone bird**, **African fish-eagle**, **grey crowned crane**, lion,
leopard, **elephant** (& tusks), rhino, buffalo, springbok/kudu/antelope, **Adinkra** signs
(Gye Nyame "supremacy of God", **Sankofa** bird/heart "return & fetch it", **Duafe** comb,
**Akoma** heart, Dwennimmen ram's-horns), **calabash / gourd**, **drum (djembe)**, **cowrie
shell**, **kente** stripe-pattern, **machete + half-gear** (labour/struggle), cocoa pod,
coffee branch, baobab, oil-palm. *(Egyptian antiquity subset: **ankh**, **Eye of Horus**,
**scarab**, **was-sceptre**, **djed pillar**, lotus & papyrus, falcon of Horus, uraeus cobra,
sun-disc of Ra, pyramid, sphinx.)*

### Indigenous American family (Mesoamerican / Andean / North American)
**eagle on a cactus (nopal) devouring a serpent** (Aztec foundation), quetzal, **Andean
condor**, **Inti / Sun of May** (rayed solar face — liberation), **smoking volcano**
(Central American), **Phrygian/liberty cap on a pole** (republican), maize/corn, cacao,
**feathered serpent** (Quetzalcoatl motif — a plumed naga), stepped-fret (greca), **thunderbird**,
**medicine wheel**, totem beasts (raven, bear, wolf, orca — Pacific NW formline), **teepee**,
**arrow / crossed arrows / arrowheads**, **calumet** (peace-pipe), cornucopia, cactus,
llama/alpaca, jaguar.

### Oceanic / Pacific family (maritime / island / sky-navigation)
**Southern Cross** (4–5 star constellation — the signature Pacific device), **frigatebird**,
**bird of paradise** (& kumul silhouette), **outrigger canoe / vaka**, **turtle / honu**,
**tapa-cloth geometric bands**, **tiki**, **koru** (Māori unfurling-fern spiral), **hei-tiki**,
**kava bowl**, **conch / triton shell**, manta ray, dolphin, palm, hibiscus, sun rising over
the sea, breadfruit.

### Cross-cultural religious & cosmological (already partly used)
cross (all forms above), **crescent**, **star** (5/6/7/8-point; **Star of David / hexagram**),
**yin-yang**, **dharma wheel**, **Khanda**, **Om**, **ankh**, **tree of life**, **sun disc**,
**spiral / triskele / triquetra**, **hamsa** (hand of Fatima), **evil-eye**, **endless /
eternal knot**, **lotus**, **menorah**, **fire altar**, **world-tree / axis mundi**.

## Part III — How this maps onto the code

`heraldry.js` currently has ~30 charges in `CHARGES`, each tagged with a `cat`
(`beast / mythic / plant / object / geom`). To grow into this library:

1. **Extend `CHARGES`** with Part I entries, keeping the `cat` tag and adding a couple of
   axes the grammar can already exploit:
   - `cat` gains: `bird`, `sea`, `human`, `celestial`, `cross`, `subordinary`.
   - Beasts/monsters gain an **attitude** set (already have a start: rampant/passant/…);
     add sejant, couchant, courant, combatant, guardant, and head-only (caboshed).
   - Small charges (mullet, crosslet, fleur, escallop, billet, roundel, bee, martlet…)
     stay flagged for **semé** and **2-and-1 / in-pale** arrangements.
   - Add **furs** (ermine, vair) as an alternate field "tincture" class, and more
     **partition lines** (dancetty, nebuly, raguly, potenty) — these multiply variety
     enormously at near-zero renderer cost.

2. **Add contextual motif FAMILIES** (Part II) as a second table, each keyed to an emergent
   predicate — never to a named civ:
   - **faith character** (from `faith.doctrine`): contemplative/cosmological → wheel, lotus,
     knot, yin-yang; militant → crossed-swords, sun-burst, zulfiqar-like blade; aniconic →
     crescent+star, calligraphic bar, geometric-only; ancestral/animist → totem beast,
     serpent, tree-of-life.
   - **biome / homeland** (from resource & climate fields): steppe → horse, shield+spear;
     desert → sun, palm, crescent, scimitar; forest → specific trees, boar, stag;
     island/coast → canoe, shell, frigatebird, southern-cross; tropical → elephant, big cat,
     plumed serpent, lotus.
   - **subsistence / economy**: pastoral → shield+spears, ram, horse; agrarian → garb,
     plough, maize; maritime-trade → ship, anchor, escallop; mining/industry → pick,
     hammer, gear, tower.
   - **canting** already puns on the *name*; keep it, drawing from the concrete Part I
     objects/beasts/plants.
   A house then draws its charge from: its stock tradition's favoured categories (universal
   pool), OR — probabilistically, when the gate fires — its faith/biome/subsistence family,
   OR a cant on its name. The **holy device** still leads only under a theocracy.

3. **Marshalling** (the flagged next layer) then combines these along real union/conquest
   history — where a quartered shield becomes a legible record of who absorbed whom.

The renderer (`tools/render_arms.mjs`) already draws ~28 glyphs; each new charge is one more
glyph function. Prioritise by frequency & legibility: the geometric/celestial/cross/plant
families are cheap and high-impact; beasts and monsters are the expensive, lower-fidelity
end (stylised silhouettes are fine and period-accurate).
