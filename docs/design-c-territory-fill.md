## LANE C0-4 — Territory-fill audit: measurement result + re-grounding design

### 1. WHAT WAS MEASURED (new instrument: tools/probe_fillaudit.mjs)
Per territory pass it replicates the step-4 target ledger of `fieldPolityTerritory` (countryTerritory.js:727-819) read-only — popCap = govPop·spanTechMul·1.18/RURAL_BIND_DENS, march = 150·logi²·r2, rateCap = EXPAND_RATE·r2·resScale·POP_FILL — and classifies every realm-pass as AT_TARGET (held ≥ target), RATE_LIM (gap > rateCap), or HEADROOM, plus budget-spent-vs-available (pass-to-pass held-load delta), wild-land density census vs the bind contour, governed-people share, shared-border pairs, and a multi-source BFS nearest-realm gap. Runs: 480/12k seed 8817 (189 s), 960/12k seed 8817 (308 s), and a one-constant counterfactual 480/12k with SIM_BIND_DENS=2000.

### 2. THE BINDING CONSTRAINT — measured, unambiguous, counterfactually confirmed
**The B2 size-target ledger is the sole binder. The growth machinery never binds.**
- 480/12k stall mix (realm-passes): AT_TARGET 877, HEADROOM 148, **RATE_LIM 0, frontier-starved 0** across the whole run. Budgets in play are 1–4 load vs rateCap 96 (960: vs 768) — EXPAND_RATE·resScale³·POP_FILL has ≥25× headroom and was never once clipped, at either grid. Cost-field walls never bind at the margin (wild frontier always adjacent, claims price at load ≈1).
- Targets are minuscule: Σtarget at 480/12k = 144 load vs ≈11,346 land-load (1.3% of the world) — and claimed land is 1.56%: **claimed% ≡ Σtarget%**. Split: popCap 100%, march 0 (logi ≈ cons² ≈ 0.01–0.09 ⇒ march=round(150·logi²) = 0 through 12k), nomadCap 0.
- Counterfactual (RURAL_BIND_DENS 8000→2000, same seed/grid/steps): claimed 1.56→**9.02%** (×5.8), governed people 32.5→63.3%, realms-with-a-land-border 26→76%, border pairs 6→23, median nearest-realm gap 4→0 tiles, war.began/2k 6→**18**, shatters 2→3, first colony.independent appears. Still RATE_LIM=0. One constant reprices the entire fill/contact/war economy; nothing else moves it.

### 3. IS THE SMALLNESS HONEST? Mostly YES at the reference grid — by history's own numbers
The equilibrium the ledger produces is: average density over a realm's territory ≈ BIND_DENS/(stm·1.18) ≈ 21,000 people/ref-tile at Bronze stm≈0.32 — and measured governed density is 20,500 (3.07M people / 150 tiles). Converting at the sim's demographic scale, that is ≈24 people/km² averaged over state territory — Sumer-range, historically right for early states. The trajectory-by-development is in the historical band: Bronze edge (480/12k) 1.56% of land claimed / 32.5% of people governed (history ~1–2% / ~15–25%); Classical edge (480/24k, long-run report) 8.9% (history 6–15%); Renaissance edge (960/50k) 28.4% (history ~30–45%). **The "71.6% stateless at 50k" headline is therefore mostly honest**: at 50k the world is Renaissance, not 1900; states in 1500 AD covered ~⅓ of land. The dishonest residuals are structural, not size:

**(a) Contact starvation is a SPACING defect, not a claimed% defect.** History's 1.5%-claimed Bronze world had war from genesis because its states were packed into shared cradles (Sumer: capitals 30–50 km apart). The sim's states are born isolated by construction: NUCLEATE_CAP_DIST = 8 ref-tiles (≈1,400 km capital-isolation ring) on top of entity spacing MIN_SETT_DIST = 8 ref-tiles — the cradle-cluster regime is UNREPRESENTABLE at current label supply. Result: 0% of realms have a land border until ~8k at 480 (26% at 12k), zero borders through 12k at 960, war.began 0 for the first 6k. **This is genuinely Tier C's label-supply problem** (flagged per brief): seats read out of the field at field-peak density are exactly what lets one dense basin hold many bordered polities.

**(b) The no-polity conflation.** 67% of people at the 480 Bronze edge (98% at 960/12k) live under NO polity at all, where history had segmentary polities (chiefdoms, tribes, confederacies) over nearly all peopled land by the classical era. The sim's "wilderness" conflates no-state with no-polity. Recommendation: do NOT bolt on a separate stratum mechanism now — the natural carrier is Tier C's labels below the state bar (chiefdom-grade labels on the popField, upgrading through the existing ORG_STATE_MIN statecraft gate), so polity-coverage becomes emergent from the same field read. Define its calibration target now (below).

**(c) The cross-grid lag is UPSTREAM of territory.** At matched step 12k: pfTot 9.45M (480) vs 4.16M (960) — the demographic engine itself runs ~2.3× leaner at the app-like grid; governed share 32.5% vs 1.8%; claimed 1.56% vs 0.08% (20×); realms 39 vs 23. The territory formula is exactly res-invariant; population growth, tech pace (entity-count-quantized), and nucleation supply are not. One concrete bootstrap bug found: **CORE_R=1 stamps a fixed 3×3 SIM-tile core at any grid** — 4× less real area at 960 — so newborn realms start below the govPop→target feedback's ignition mass (held med 1–4 sim tiles, target 1–2 load, feedback never lights). Every other reach quantity scales by resScale; the core stamp should too.

**(d) The late-game fill driver is unmeasured.** march = 150·logi²·r2 with logi≈cons² early (effectively quartic in construction) is the only mechanism that partitions sparse land, plus CLAIM_CAP fading with construction. It first appears at 960/10k (march 5–10 load). Whether it delivers the Industrial endgame (→ full partition of habitable land) inside the honest tech pace is untested — define it as a Tier C calibration gate rather than pre-tuning the constant.

### 4. THE RE-GROUNDING (what to change, what to refuse to change)
1. **Do NOT touch EXPAND_RATE / POP_FILL / resScale³ / claim-cost constants.** They never bind (0 rate-clipped realm-passes in 3 runs, 2 grids); retuning them would be painting a non-cause. The reckoning's "EXPAND_RATE/POP_FILL scale audit" is hereby CLOSED with data: the resScale³ rate is correct in form and irrelevant in practice.
2. **Pay B2's own recorded unit debt (risk 3 of design-size-target-regrounding.md), as a derivation, not a tune.** RURAL_BIND_DENS=8000 was derived from pre-B3 demography (pfTot 13.7M@480/12k, "0.07× Earth"); post-B3 pfTot@12k is 9.45M (×0.69) and matched-development population is lower still. Re-derive the physical band in post-B3 units at matched development and re-run the B2 validation-3 sweep (5000/8000/12000 → likely ~3000/5000/8000) with acceptance = claimed%-by-development AND governed-share-by-development inside the historical bands (§5) on ≥2 seeds — never "more fill is better." Expected honest landing ~4,000–6,000 → ×1.3–2 fill at every development level; the ×5.8 of the 2000 counterfactual overshoots history at Bronze (9% land / 63% people) and is evidence, not a recommendation.
3. **Fix the CORE_R resolution variance** (one constant: stamp radius ×resScale, i.e. a fixed REAL-area stability floor) — a mechanism-true res-invariance repair of the fill bootstrap at the app grid.
4. **Land the fill-economy instrument permanently**: fold this probe's channels (Σtarget vs held + stall mix, governed-people share, border pairs, nearest-realm gap) into diag_full so the next deep run measures the fill economy by development level, closing the long-run report's follow-ups #1/#6 measurement side.
5. **Everything else is Tier C's, by design**: seat/label supply at field density (fixes spacing/contact/war-candidacy and the realm-count ceiling), light-polity labels below the state bar (fixes the no-polity conflation), label-dense member flow (unsticks captured=0/annexed=0 — with realms of 1–3 members, war is lethal-or-nothing because there is almost never a non-capital member to transfer).

### 5. TIER C CALIBRATION TARGETS (the numbers Tier C must hit; all gated on emergent development of the leading edge — era read from tech, never step/year)
- **claimed% of land**: Bronze 1–3% (HEAD 480: 1.56 PASS) · Classical 6–15% (HEAD: 8.9 PASS) · Medieval 15–30% · Renaissance 25–45% (HEAD 960: 28.4 PASS-low) · Industrial ≥85% of habitable. Tier C must NOT regress these — they are currently healthy at the reference grid.
- **governed-people share** (people under any STATE): Bronze 15–35% (HEAD 480: 32.5 PASS; 960: 1.8 FAIL) · Classical 40–65% · Renaissance 70–90% · Industrial ≥97%. **polity-covered share** (incl. light polities, once labels exist): ≥80% of people from the Classical edge on.
- **contact**: ≥40% of realms with ≥1 land border at the Bronze edge (HEAD: 26% at 480, 0% at 960), ≥70% Classical+; median nearest-realm BFS gap ≤4 ref-tiles at Bronze, ≤1 at Classical.
- **war candidacy**: war.began ≥0.05/realm/1k on EVERY seed at Classical+ (the stylized floor), achieved via border supply, never via lowering war bars.
- **member/label flow**: settlement (label) transfers between LIVING realms >0 per 10k steps at Classical+ (HEAD: 0 in 24k).
- **label supply itself**: labels per unit REAL area tracks local field density — a Sumer-density basin supports seats at ~2–4 ref-tile spacing (5–15 polities per cradle at Bronze); planet label count grows with population, not pinned ~90–195 at every grid.
- **cross-grid invariance**: every band above within ×1.5 between the 240-ref and 480-sim grids at matched development (today ×2.5–20 apart), with pfTot-at-matched-development itself a watched channel (the divergence is upstream demography, not territory).

2. 12k horizon: march-driven late fill and Classical+ contact at 480 are extrapolated from the long-run report's 24k/50k numbers, not re-measured here (CPU budget); the diag_full extension (validation 4) is the closure.
3. Governed-share historical bands (§5) are scholarly estimates with wide error bars (pre-modern population under states is contested); treat as soft bands, hard only at the extremes (960's 1.8% at Bronze-edge is unambiguously wrong; 32.5% vs "15–25%" is not).
4. Sweeping RURAL_BIND_DENS down interacts with war snowballing and realm count (bd2000 arm: realms 39→30 — bigger states absorb founding space) and with the pfTot↑ coupling (state coverage feeds population) — the sweep must watch realm-count and largest-share gates, per B2's own risk list.
5. The popField's spatial concentration (flatter than real history's cradle-heavy distribution) means claimed% and governed-share cannot BOTH be pushed to historical values by any single density constant — if the sweep can't satisfy both bands, the finding is a popField-concentration mechanism gap (surface it, don't force the constant).
6. Two live probes wrote ~30 min of shared CPU; the probe file tools/probe_fillaudit.mjs is committed-quality but uncommitted — parent should decide whether it lands with the lane.
