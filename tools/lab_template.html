Emblem Genome

One gene vector, expressed as a flat banner. Shape it with the design axes, then evolve it — reroll, mutate, breed. Every emblem is the phenotype of its genes; drift them and the design drifts with them. No pattern is locked to a "kind of realm" — every tradition is reachable by any genome.

Design axes

Abstract dials — they bias the genome without locking any pattern to a kind of realm. Move one and the emblem re-forms.
