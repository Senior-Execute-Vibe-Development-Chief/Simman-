A heraldic genetics lab

Emblem Genome

One gene vector, expressed as a flat banner. Shape it with the design axes, then evolve it — reroll, mutate, breed. Every emblem is the phenotype of its genes; drift them and the design drifts with them. No pattern is locked to a "kind of realm" — every tradition is reachable by any genome.

Style presets

Bookmarks into style-space, named for the historical traditions they evoke. They only set genes — nothing is locked to a culture: evolve freely from any of them, and any genome can still become anything.

Design axes

Abstract dials — they bias the genome without locking any pattern to a kind of realm. Move one and the emblem re-forms. Note: axes re-found the genome from its seed, so shape first, then evolve.
