The line of descent

Left to right: the most basic form through to the flag as it stands. Click any ancestor to return to it — evolving onward branches a new line from there.

How it works. A flag here is the expression of a small gene vector. Roll a founder and the founding reads your sliders as soft leans on that vector (the same mapping the simulation uses to give a realm arms from its biome, temperament, wealth and faith). Drift mutates the vector; Succeed passes it to an heir with a mark of difference; Unite recombines two houses, quartering their arms — heraldry's real marshalling, as genetic crossover. Most basic strips it back to a plain field so you can watch complexity accumulate from nothing.
