# 14 — Discovery and colonization  `[DESIGN DETAIL — ratified DECISIONS 11]`

The map is not known by its inhabitants. Exploration is investment in
pushing a knowledge frontier; colonies are what expeditions *become* when
they land, never what they are declared to be.

## 14.1 The known world (11a)

- **Geographic knowledge** is a knowledge-domain application (11):
  per maritime/cultural tradition (coarse — never per-center masks), a
  known-world frontier with fidelity decaying over relay distance —
  surveyed home waters → traded coasts → reported shores → rumor → fog.
  Pilots are carriers; sailing directions and charts are writing-domain
  artifacts that let geographic knowledge survive its carriers; print
  later fuses traditions' knowledge continent-wide.
- **Nothing unknown can be traded with, sailed to, or coveted.**
  Exploration hazard/investment rises with the value of the *known-to-
  exist* beyond reach — and the historical trigger emerges from existing
  books: chokepoint tolls (06) on overland luxury routes raise the price
  gradient toward finding another way; whoever holds ocean technique,
  port capital, and a chartering center goes looking.
- **The mappa mundi lens** (owner feature, 11a): render the world as a
  tradition believes it — crisp known coasts; far shores deterministically
  warped by their actual report-chain quality; speculative fill; honest
  fog. No-lies compatible: it renders belief, labeled as belief (12.2).

## 14.2 First contact (11c)

Post-glacial isolation persists by ocean cost alone (the prehistoric
peopling crossed by land — the ancestry wavefront covers it). Contact is
the collision of divergent branches: crop packages jump oceans (maize and
potatoes reshaping Old World capacity — a first-order demographic event),
techniques transfer with carriers (11), and the disease asymmetry (15,
plagues) does the tragic work — pool depth ∝ historic density + livestock
+ connectivity; virgin-soil mortality ∝ pool difference; the malaria wall
runs the other way. Timing, direction, and discoverer vary by seed.

## 14.3 Colonies as outcomes (11b)

An expedition that lands becomes what three local facts make it:

| Local facts | Outcome |
|---|---|
| dense organized natives + valuable goods + deadly disease | **trading post** — fortified factory at an existing port; a charter edge + a tiny community |
| thin natives + settler-viable climate/crops/disease | **settler colony** — migration wave; new communities; identity drift into creole peoples (07, unmodified) |
| profits pull, disease blocks settlement | **extraction colony** — plantations on coerced labor (06.4, grimly) |
| organized-but-outgunned courts + naval projection | **protectorate** — v1's validated gunboat mechanism as imposed obligation edges |

No menu, no types in code — the same fleet becomes different things on
different coasts.

## 14.4 Control across oceans (11d)

The monitoring frontier's final exam: months-distant edges are maximally
weak, so colonial rule runs through chartered delegates who drift
autonomous (05's delegation physics, unmodified). The v1-validated
independence rule ports: the dependency breaks away when local capacity
outgrows the metropole's *projected* force (13's decay law). Colonial
economy (investment young, tribute mature, technique transfer with
carriers) ports onto the edge/books substrate. Decolonization is the
monitoring frontier failing across an ocean.
