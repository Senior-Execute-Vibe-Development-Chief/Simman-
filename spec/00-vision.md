# 00 — Vision

## What this is

A comprehensive, utterly realistic global history simulator. It runs on real
Earth — real terrain, real observed climate, real crop biogeography — and
lets twelve thousand years of history emerge from physics and principles:
people spread, farm, and starve; markets form where routes cross; power
condenses where grain can be taken and flight is blocked; chiefs become
lords, lords become kings, city-states become empires, and empires rot back
into the countryside — none of it scripted, staged, or propped up.

**The differentiator:** every other history sim is staged — scripted starts,
railed outcomes, nation-states assumed from turn one. This one produces
**plausible alternate Earths**. Rome may never rise; something Rome-shaped
will rise somewhere, at roughly the right frequency, for the right reasons.
Reality is our test set, never our script (DECISIONS 1, 5).

## The player fantasy (Phase 1)

There is no player-actor in Phase 1 (DECISIONS 4). Phase 1 is the
observatory done right: a fully zoomable living Earth — from the whole
planet down through realms, valleys, and towns to the rendered villages and
people implied by the fields — with lenses for politics, peoples, tongues,
faiths, and economy; chronicles the world writes about itself; and a seed
that IS a complete alternate history you can share, replay, and compare.
The product of a run is a world and its history.

## Phases

- **Phase 1 (this spec): Earth, Younger Dryas → early modern.** Start at
  t=0 = end of the Younger Dryas (~9700 BCE). End defined by development,
  never date: ocean navigation, print, gunpowder armies, proto-state
  administration — the birth of the hard-bordered state is Phase 1's climax.
- **Phase 2: the industrial machine.** Fossil energy enters the (already
  energy-conserving) books; exponential growth, mass politics, the
  demographic transition. Planned for; not built in Phase 1 — but Phase 1's
  ledgers must not preclude it (the organic-energy seam, 04/06).
- **Phase 3+: speculative future; procedural planets.** Earthness lives
  only in data (01-constitution R7), so procedural worlds are a data swap,
  not an architecture change.
- **Game phases:** player agency layers on later, as pressures entering the
  same fields the emergent actors read. Out of scope here.

## What we keep from v1

The deep verticals port: the worldgen pipeline (terrain, climate solvers,
rivers, crops, resources — certified portable, research/05), the language
engine (naming, scripts, drift — four-verb interface), the emblem/heraldry
genome, the validation harness and its methodology, and every mechanism the
dossiers mark "port verbatim" (research/04 §5). What does not come is the
entity-country ontology, the dual representations, and the lever swamp.

## What success looks like

A 30-minute session: generate a seed, watch farming crawl out of the real
hearths, watch the first tribute arrows appear in the caged valleys, watch a
city rise on the Euphrates and a chain of lords assemble behind it, scrub
forward through its empire's rings, its famines, its succession crisis and
fall — zoom into one village that outlived all of it — then check the
almanac and find the world's population curve, city sizes, and empire
statistics sitting inside real history's bands. Then share the seed.
