the world population curve inside the historical envelope, regional

## Rulings new in this handoff (spec authority, pre-answered)

1. **The tick is one month.** Twelve ticks per year, fixed timestep; the
   M1 calendar counter is now the sim clock. Rates are expressed per
   year in constants and converted at the tick. Heavy passes may declare
   an annual cadence (performance scheduling only, R1); any pass whose
   stride measurably changes trajectory declares stride=1 as its
   contract (the POP_FIELD_STRIDE scar, 02).
2. **`people` is stored as DENSITY — persons per km² — per cell.**
   Resolution-invariant by construction; totals derive via each cell's
   real area (the M1 row geometry gives it); the conservation ledger
   runs in PERSONS. There is no sim-unit scale, no POP_SCALE, no census
   copy anywhere.
3. **Initial condition**: forager-capacity equilibrium (a ledgered fill
   fraction of forager capacity) masked by the substrate's deep-ancestry
   peopling extent — humans start everywhere the pre-Holocene wavefront
   reached, and NOWHERE else. Remote islands (the Pacific, Madagascar,
   New Zealand, Iceland) stay empty until discovery mechanics exist;
   their emptiness is a fact, not a bug.
4. **The M0 placeholder tick and noise field die now.** People is the
   first real conserved quantity; the smoke suite's determinism,
   save/load, and conservation checks run on the real field set.
   `SAVE_VERSION` bumps to 3 (people enters `FIELD_LIST`; the substrate
   is still never saved).
5. **Migration reads the travel field's own local costs.** The
   capacity-gradient diffusion's per-edge conductance derives from the
   M1 foot-mode edge cost (cheap valley = high conductance, mountain
   wall = low), via a narrow read API on the cost module — NOT a second
   terrain formula (R4; "no other reach formulas anywhere"). Long-range
   CRP queries are not needed for diffusion; local edge costs are.
6. **Conservation channels for people**: births (source), deaths (sink),
   and migration, which must NET TO ZERO within the pass — the
   double-buffered gather form makes that provable. Per-pass aggregated
   accounting as established at M1.
7. **Demographic regimes port with their v1 grounding.** Intrinsic
   growth ≈0.28%/yr, modulated r_i = r × (R_DEV0 + R_DEVK·dev_i) ÷
   (1 + R_TROPIC·burden_i) — forager land ×0.35, advanced farming
   ×1.65 (the differential that drives the Neolithic wave); the disease
   burden is the ported climate-STATE habitability brake (static at M2;
   dynamic pools are chapter 16, M7+). The urban graveyard —
   density-graded excess mortality — ports re-founded in real units
   (persons/km² thresholds, ledgered), active from day one.
8. **The technique wave at M2 is FARMING technique**: one land-anchored
   field (0..1), only ever rising at the field level, spreading at the
   measured ~1 km/yr, climate-tolled through the crop packages'
   tolerances (axes emerge — never a named-region factor, R2). Hearth
   ignition ports v1's law: real Old-World hearth pins as data + the
   cradle scorer, each armed hearth accruing peopled-basin years against
   its package's domestication lag; the first to cross ignites. No
   calendar gate anywhere (R1). Craft/administrative knowledge as living
   populations is chapter 11, M8 — do not build it.
9. **Age structure**: three cohort-fraction fields (children / working /
   elders) riding the people field, evolved by a fixed-rate compartment
   flow from births, deaths, and aging; fractions sum to 1 (asserted).
   Nothing consumes them yet beyond the collector — they exist because
   M3's labor and later war losses read them, and retrofitting them
   into saves is worse than carrying them now.
10. **Neutral slots, documented and inert**: works (build/rot phase of
    the ported kernel stays off), soil fatigue, deforestation — they
    arm at M3+ with the food economy. The travel field's capability
    sets remain query parameters (mode capabilities belong to the
    M8 knowledge system, not the farming wave).
11. **Tools may cache the substrate per (seed, preset, grid) within a
    process.** The gate/bench/oracle rebuild it repeatedly; a
    memoized `buildSubstrate` wrapper in tools (never in sim state) is
    legitimate and keeps CI wall-clock sane.
12. **The shell's long-load work lands here**: the target-grid build
    moves off the UI thread into the worker (snapshot protocol), with a
    real progress indicator, and the built substrate is cached in OPFS
    keyed by (seed, config digest, grid, code version) — evictable and
    rebuildable, so it does not violate the never-store-terrain
    doctrine. First target load pays the minute once; reopening is
    seconds.

    Note (2026-09-01, Decision 20): the Earth raster was regenerated
    from real ETOPO1 (the inherited mask drowned every low coastal
    plain — Sumer, Bengal, the deltas). The M2 hearth/density gates run
    against the corrected coastline; any substrate cache MUST key on
    data identity (the config digest above covers it) so stale drowned
    maps never serve.

## Deliverables

### 1. The `people` field and its kernels — `src/sim/people/`

Port the four popFieldKernel phases onto the substrate, re-founded in
real units, minus the works phase (ruling 10):

- **Capacity** (derived each pass, never stored): forager baseline from
  the ported habitability/biogeography physics where the wave has not
  arrived; farmed capacity = fertility × technique reached × water-access
  bands × relief, in persons/km², constants ledgered with their v1
  grounding rows. The v1 kernel's per-tile constants convert to real
  units at the world's own km-per-cell (band convention for 1-D
  river/coast terms).
- **Growth**: logistic toward capacity at the regime-modulated rate
  (ruling 7), monthly-converted.
- **Migration**: the double-buffered, sub-stepped, index-ordered gather
  kernel, conductance from the travel field (ruling 5), rate anchored to
  v1's measured pre-modern mobility correction.
- **Cohorts** (ruling 9).

Keep the band-kernel SHAPE (pure functions over cell ranges, scan-then-
apply) even though worker-pool parallelism itself is optional at M2 —
measure single-thread first; if budget forces banding, the v1
bit-identity contract applies verbatim.

### 2. The technique wave — `src/sim/people/technique.ts`

Ruling 8. The field enters `FIELD_LIST` (saved); hearth pins are a
cited data table (`data/reality/hearths.json`: location, package,
domestication-lag source). Spread interacts with climate through package
tolerances only. The wave feeds capacity (deliverable 1) and the growth
regime (ruling 7).

### 3. Initial peopling

Ruling 3, applied at world creation after the substrate builds: fill
fraction × forager capacity over the ancestry extent; ledgered as the
opening balance of the people sheet.

### 4. Conservation, save, hash, smoke

Rulings 4 and 6. Smoke's determinism/save-load/continuation runs move to
the real fields at both grids; the conservation sheet asserts zero
unexplained flux on people every audited tick, with births/deaths/
migration channels visible in the snapshot. The collector picks up the
new fields automatically — verify `pop.people` (real persons, world
total), `pop.perKm2`, largest-cell density, technique coverage share,
and cohort shares appear and are perturbation-reachable.

### 5. The shell grows — population on screen

- **Population lens**: log-scaled density; the world must visibly fill
  from the hearths outward over a run.
- **Technique lens**: the farming wave's extent.
- **Run controls**: run/pause and a speed multiplier driving the worker;
  a live world-population readout (persons) with the sim date derived
  from step (display only, R1).
- The worker-thread substrate build + progress + OPFS cache (ruling 12).

## What NOT to do (recap)

No food books, harvest years, granaries, famine, storage, or fish (M3).
No communities register, no unrest/legitimacy books (M3/M5). No herds or
pasture stocks (15; M3). No dynamic disease pools (16). No craft/
knowledge system beyond the farming wave (11; M8). No armies, no
politics. Never gate anything on step, year, or era (R1) — the hearth
law and the wave carry all timing from state. Never dial a constant to
land a curve checkpoint (R2) — miss honestly and manifest it. No second
population representation of any kind — no matter how convenient a
cached census looks (R4; v1's costliest lesson). No per-tile constants —
real units at the world's own km-per-cell (R3).
