## 1. FINAL DESIGNS THAT WON

### 1.1 Polity / territory

**The field is the political ontology; settlements are dressing.** `world._countryOwner` (an Int32 per tile) is the authored, persisted political state; political events (war, secession, absorption) move TILES; `s.countryId` is DERIVED — "a settlement belongs to whoever owns the ground under it" (field-polity-spec.md, `FIELD_POLITY=1`). Under `TILE_POLITY=1` only the CAPITAL anchors/pins political ground ("Non-capital cities ride the ground … but never pin, seed, or create political land"); under `CATCHMENT_CLIP=1` the economic catchment is a *reactive read* of the political map — "settlements should be entirely reactionary to the country's tiles and borders, never affecting them" (owner directive, field-polity-spec.md). `CATCH_WILD=1` closes the accident where "STATEHOOD CONFISCATES THE FIELDS AT THE VILLAGE DOOR" (tuning.js CATCH_WILD desc): a settlement may work its own country's ground or wilderness, never a foreign realm's.

**Expansion = budgeted frontier growth, not Voronoi.** Each territory pass a polity spends a tile budget `EXPAND_RATE(8) × r2 × resScale × POP_FILL(12)` on the cheapest frontier tiles over the terrain-cost field (relief/desert/sea gate it naturally); over-target realms shed most-remote-first, never worked/home land (field-polity-spec.md v1 results, empire-consolidation comboE).

**Size target (the empire-size law, after five generations of failures):**
```
popCap = govPop(own realm) × spanTechMul(own statecraft) × ADMIN_LOAD_RECAL × r2 / RURAL_BIND_DENS
target = popCap × (1 + MARCH_FUNDED(2) × logistics²)          // MARCH_FUNDED, v33
+ SIZE_WORKED=1: base also counts people on ground the realm's members already WORK but don't hold
  → target = k·h + W/bindDens, stable interior fixed point at h* = W/(bindDens·(1−k))
+ floor for small realms: max()'d with COVER_BASE(25) + COVER_ORG(260)·org ref-tiles
```
`RURAL_BIND_DENS` is the ONLY unit in the target — physical, frozen, "never a live statistic" (design-size-target-regrounding.md), now 5500 (= the ratified 8000 × 0.69 demographic rescale after B3, per tuning.js). The march is a *multiple of the people-funded extent*, not a flat technique allowance — "a city-state with Roman roads never held 0.4M km2 of steppe" (MARCH_FUNDED desc). `FIELD_SPAN=6` caps capacity-grown extent.

**War: force projection is local.** Effective might at any front tile = national might × `exp(−d/H)`, d from the realm's own capital, `H = WAR_REACH(15) ref-tiles × (1 + 2.2·logisticsLevel)` — exponential, not hyperbolic, "since per-march-day supply loss is multiplicative and no hyperbolic tail inverts a 10-20× might gap at this grid" (empire-consolidation-2026-07.md, THE IMMORTAL-GIANTS FIX). The storm is fought at the capital's distance against the capital's OWN garrison (fixing the adapter that "accidentally garrisoned the walls with the whole national pool"). Applied at fronts, countryside, amphibious beaches, attrition, storms. Result: "every map axis improves, the frozen top-5 finally churns."

**Succession/restoration (`SUCCESSOR_STATES=1`, design-successor-states.md):** a wilderness founding restores a fallen polity iff (1) ≥2/3 of the basin's remembered people-weight points at ONE fallen nation (`RESTORE_MAJORITY`), (2) the remembered mass alone clears the ordinary founding bar (`NUCLEATE_CLUSTER_POP × capMul`, no new constant), (3) the polity is actually dead, (4) ≥1/2 of the seat's census descends from the fallen realm's founding culture (`RESTORE_KIN`, lineal walk over `parentCultureId`). Successor seats are label-free: "(tier ≥ CITY_TIER) || (_provinceCity === self)" — the locally-strongest member per PROVINCE_SPAN(9 ref-tiles), plus a ≥2-members-per-successor fold. Silent sheds become witnessed `polity.receded` events (`resolveOrphanedMarches`).

**State birth:** statecraft symmetry — `ORG_STATE_MIN(0.15)` gates founding AND a realm's adoption/annexation of independents (country-count-size-diagnosis.md fix #2). De-novo statehood waits for the Writing bar (`STATE_RECORDS`, v37); the basin's people decide birth (`SEAT_FIELD`, v42); the pre-urban org engine is Carneiro caging with the pressure leg: drive × basin demographic fill through the "flight knee" — "pressure 0 while >20% of basin capacity is free — a defeated village flees and re-sows" (`CAGE_FILL`, v49). Mountain ranges exist politically via the **relief field** (max−min elevation over 3×3, max-pooled to sim tiles): post-fix the Alps cost 108.7 vs a Paris→Frankfurt plain march 35.7 (was 37.7 ≈ equal). Enclave-swallowing pays the same statecraft bars as absorption.

### 1.2 Population

**One population** (one-population.md, ruling adopted; `ONE_POP=1`, `POP_FIELD=1` default): the people-on-land field `popField` is the only stock; a settlement is "a high concentration of that same population"; `s.people` is a derived VIEW = Σ popField(catchment) × `_onePopScale`. Capacity per tile: `capBand = fert × CAP_PER_FERT × dev × access × relief × industry × works` with the water-transport premium multiplicative on fert (`ACCESS_RIVER/COAST` growing with leading agriculture) and the relief penalty `1/(1+RELIEF_PEN·relief)`. Logistic growth + capacity-seeking 4-neighbour migration, double-buffered, per-REAL-area (`CAP_PER_FERT`, `SEED_POP` ÷ rNormPop² — the res-invariance arc). Genesis seeds at `DAWN=0.35` of Malthusian equilibrium ("the long dawn" — a rolling dawn instead of a genesis pulse, budget-gated-expansion.md).

**Urbanisation** is the v55 "12k abolish stack", all default-on: `CORE_LOCAL` (a city's own harvest counts toward its urban core — `coreEff = min(_coreF, max(holdF, kLocal) + kBeyond)`, `max` so no core can fall), `AGGLOM_LOCAL`, `URBAN_LABOR` (a farmer who urbanises stops farming — "the first brake that costs nothing", tier-ratchet §13), `STAMP_RETIRE` (the 12k founding stamp no longer floors size), `VIABLE_UNITS` (the `K_MIN_VIABLE=8` floor reads 8 *people*, not the unit-bug 8,000), `DISSOLVE_CORE=0.2`. The Zipf tail mechanism is agglomeration vs congestion: urban total set by the economy (Σ import-fed capacity), distributed by β-compressed shares, with the congestion brake grounded in the **urban graveyard** — excess mortality scaled `×(coreDensity/medianCoreDensity)^γ` (`URBAN_AGGLOM=0.13`, `URBAN_GAMMA=0.5`) so "size ∝ economy^(1/(1+γ)) falls out of a real death rate" (one-population.md). `URBAN_FOOD_GATE` (v64) caps the agglomeration pull when moving a farmer would leave the food flow short of core need.

### 1.3 Settlement register

**The register is the CITY register.** "A SETTLEMENT IS A CITY — or a community growing into one. NEVER a mere village or market town" (owner directive 2026-08-05; `DISSOLVE_TOWNS`, `CITY_CORE`, `DISSOLVE_FARMS` all default 1). Villages/towns are "implied in the land" — popField + each city's `_ruralPop`. Mint at a ~10,000-person urban core; dissolve at 2,000 — the owner's hysteresis: "*mint 10,000 dissolve 2000 … dissolving at 2000 just gives places that used to be cities some breathing room*" (tier-ratchet §30), implemented as `DISSOLVE_CORE = 0.2 × TIER_CORE[2]` = the codebase's own town bar. Tier (village/town/city/metropolis, `TIER_CORE=[0,2,10,40]`) is a READOUT only — every capability table keyed on it was retired at v51 (§1.4). Tier bar derivation: `TIER_BRANCH=4` (city bar = K towns' load, Christaller K) replaced both absolute floors and rank quotas.

**Where cities come from:** agriculture has multiple origins — `MULTI_HEARTH=1`: the Earth hearth pins become a pre-load, not a cap; the emergent cradle scorer (`f*2 + riverBonus + landBarrier*2.5 + tempFit + elevFit − seaFrac term`, Carneiro circumscription) fills the rest, `CRADLE_MIN_SEP` read as a REAL distance (design-c-hearth-field.md; flipped 2026-08-03; hearth count 7, invariant 240/480/960). Invention is staggered (`INVENT_STAGGER=1`: maturity T = 6500y/score); towns follow the technique front (`INDEP_TECH=1`: founding probability × `min(1, devField/NEOLITHIC_AGRI)` — "a town is something the package does when it arrives"; Siberia's first founding moved step 144 → 1824, tech~cradle-distance correlation −0.90). Founding rate scales with basin people (`CROWD_FOUND`), mint requires unmarketed residual basin mass and day-one reach (`MINT_RESIDUAL`, `MINT_REACH`, v57), newborn seed boxes obey ownership (`SEED_EXCLUSIVE`, v57), and `INVENT_JUMP` (v62/63) opens the dawn at the first mint-ready site.

### 1.4 Food economy

**The two-mode model** is the frame (food-system-design-2026-08-27.md §1): "Food moves for one of two reasons: someone PAID for it, or someone TOOK it… Mode M stops when the freight eats the spread… Mode C stops when the claim stops being enforceable." Six flows exist (own catchment; levy child→liege at `LEVY_MAX=0.7×foodReach`, unpaid, intra-realm; liege coin purchase; peer market `GRAIN_MARKET`; tribute-of-land skimmed off field people at `TRIB_FOOD_PER_POP(0.0030)×TRIBUTE_RATE(0.10)×extract(0.3→1.0·org)`; `TRIBUTE_UP=0.33` cross-realm; court exchange at fixed customary ratios; colony subsidy). `FARM_RENT(0.4)` is fiscal only — "priced off the harvest but paid from s.wealth."

**The winner for land assignment is the bid-rent race** (`MARKET_PULL=1`, v51 — "THE FARM-FIELD OVERHAUL… the owner's rule: 'a farmed tile sends food to whoever will pay better, or whoever is easier, or whoever forces them to'"). Mechanism, per the tuning.js desc and tier-ratchet §44-50:
- Same multi-source Dijkstra, different starting potential: `seed = −haulTiles·(bid_i/meanBid)` instead of 0, **no budget** — "reach is what a market wins, never an allowance it is granted."
- Bid = hunger × ability to pay: `_scarcity` (demand/supply on the production-relative book, `PRICE_GROSS=1`) × spare coin (`MARKET_PAY`, v65: `min(willingness, ability)`), each normalised by the world mean — zero new constants. Deliberately NOT `_grainPrice` (which carried the tier table).
- **Finite range**: delivered value = A − freight·d, hits zero; frontier stops at `nd ≥ 0`. Derived from `HAUL_LAND_KM=340`; the zero point is `D = 2×340 = 680 km` (e-folding conversion). Range grows with the shipping region's tech (roads + wagons + the cons>0.85 rail/refrigeration leap — `FOOD_HAUL_TECH`).
- Carriage is a shared neutral cost over real terrain **including roads and ports** (the claimant-dependent cost was removed — "moving grain over a pass costs what it costs whoever buys it").
- Both straight-line geometric claim phases (Chebyshev core block, Euclidean hinterland Voronoi) are gone; owned land is re-contestable — a field can switch buyer each pass.
- Cross-border bidding: grain may be bought across borders without moving the border (money-field header: "MARKET_PULL=1 bid-sized catchments with cross-border trade… 13.8% cross-border farmed tiles" at 960/28k).
- `LAND_SURPLUS` (v54): the implied countryside EATS FIRST — `surplus = max(0, grossFood − mouths×0.003)` — only surplus enters the bid ("high fertility (Nile) with the same farmers yields large surplus automatically, not a fixed fraction").
- Retired as capability drivers (v51): `CORE_BY_TIER`, `HINTERLAND_BY_TIER`, `FOOD_RANGE_BY_TIER`, `GRAIN_PRICE_BY_TIER`, plus `reachBudget/ORG_REACH/TERRITORY_BASE` — "the economic catchment at the register that ships has no live size term at all" is the measurement that convicted them (reachBudget max/p50 = 1.02-1.05; ρ(grain price, catchment tiles) = −0.011; a plain Euclidean Voronoi reproduces the live partition moving 14.2% of tiles).

**The haul physics** (v50 grain shed): `HAUL_PHYS` — land e-folds at 340 real km (Diocletian's Price Edict), water ×12 only over an actual sea-lane ROUTE; `GRAIN_FREIGHT` — the buyer buys at the farm gate and the road eats the loss; `GRAIN_BID` — scarce grain to the highest scarcity price, not founding order. Plus `ONE_BOOK`+`SHIP_SURPLUS` (v45, one food book; the farm gate sells the surplus, not a tier slice), `GRAIN_PROVISION` (v48, the annona), `HARVEST_YEARS`+`LEAN_YEAR` (v46, real annual swings; founding bars at each basin's own deep-year margin), `GRANARY_SPOIL`+`CLIMATE_SPOIL` (v66), `MARKET_FARM_HOLD` (v67). The partition stays **exclusive** — "A field is in one manor, one parish, one contado. Bracton's 6⅔-mile rule is a rule *enforcing* exclusivity" (gravity-partition-memo §2) — but exists as assignment, not allowance ("Cities need a catchment in the sense of 'each field feeds one market.' They do not need a catchment sized by a technology budget", tier-ratchet §38.3).

**Land productivity:** the fitted `_eraProd = 1 + 260·agri⁶·devGate` overlay was retired; productivity = `worksMul × indCap`, where `worksMul = 1 + LAND_WORKS(2)·_terrWorksMean` (Boserupian works built by population pressure × water × technique, harvest-weighted over the farmed catchment) and `s._indCap` is the industrial break (~10-30×) — "the ledger and the field finally agree on what the industrial break is worth" (design-food-economy-wave.md). Crops are evaluated on **the season they grow in** (`GROW_SEASON=1`): each crop classified warm/cool-season by its own tOpt (boundary 0.80 ≈ 18°C), read against the half-year's temperature and rainfall — "the maize blanket over the Old-World cradles breaks."

**Fish:** removed as a food source entirely — owner directive 2026-08-14, `FISH def 0`: "Coastal food is land food alone; the coast keeps its TRADE and NAVAL meaning… only the calories go." (The full Schaefer labor/stock fishery of design-food-economy-wave Part B was built first and remains behind the lever.)

### 1.5 Money / markets

**Specie → mint → circulation → credit** (currency-system.md, all phases default-on): mines yield bullion; the state mint strikes it (`face = bullion/fineness`, `SEIGNIORAGE_RATE=0.05` to treasury); **debasement** is the war-finance lever, self-limiting ("seigniorage ∝ the fineness DROP, so it self-limits at the floor"), its cost landing abroad via terms-of-trade FX (`fxRate = seller fineness ÷ buyer fineness`, capped [0.8, 1.3] "so debasement bites without a death-spiral"). **Hume price-specie-flow**: seller volume scaled by `(P_buyer/P_seller)^HUME_ELASTICITY(0.5)` — measured: max localP 2.03→1.36, "specie self-distributes." Total supply bounded by finite deposits + `COIN_LOSS_RATE` (the honest replacement for the freight burn) + credit contraction. **The (b) nominal-inflation model** is the load-bearing resolution: real decisions (grain price, building cost, army wages) use BASE prices, never × localP; localP is kept only for Hume and display — "inflation no longer SQUEEZES the domestic economy." Credit is gated on the discrete banking TECH and ships at `CREDIT_RATE=0.01` (the 3-seed flip was blocked by the 4242 market-integration canary and `_credit` wear-drift).

**The per-tile money field** (`TILE_MONEY=1`, v52; money-field-2026-08-28.md): "Coin sits on the tile that sold the grain; it leaves through the same forces that made peasants sell — tax, rent, salt, tithe — gated on the world they live in, not the calendar." One coin, one holder (buyer wealth − → `_tileWealth[ti]` +; the market seat never double-books); sinks are pull × stock × gate demand curves (land tax in coin via tile monetization; rent/tithe on the tile's harvest attribution; inelastic salt/iron necessity purchases; faith tithe with pilgrim distance decay; coin loss); unserved coin outside any bid ring is a founding pull. This "makes the bid real: a bid with no payee is an accounting fiction."

### 1.6 Labor

**Coerced labor, default-on** (coerced-labor.md): the `_unfree` stock does what free population structurally cannot — decouple labor from amenity, divert land to cash crops (`_cashFrac` gated on cash-crop suitability × food security), intensify mining, be a trade commodity. Supply = capture on sack/raid (`CAPTURE_FRAC`) + slaver middlemen; demand = plantations, mines, and — the crucial addition — **latifundia**: extraction income (`gov._conqFlow` = plunder + tribute, decaying over ~2 generations) → `conqShare` → estate formation on a tenure RATCHET ("consolidation is fast… fragmentation slow — the Gracchi died on that") → `labourDemand += ESTATE_PULL × _estates`, which is why the temperate conquest core buys gang labor (measured: without it the classical world sat ~10% coerced vs Rome's 30-40%). One scarcity index per trade component drives price AND slaving effort (`SLAVE_PULL` — "supply responds to demand the way the Roman and Atlantic systems actually scaled"); "when expansion stops, plunder and captives dry together… slavery ages into serfdom with no era gate" (the colonate). Serfdom = coercive `FARM_RENT` with the plague fork (weak lord → wages rise; strong coercion + export pull → second serfdom). Free labor: `URBAN_LABOR` withdraws urbanised farmers from the harvest 1:1.

### 1.7 Goods

**The 8-good vector economy** (economy-goods-vector-spec.md), the whole staircase now default-on: "One price per settlement → a vector of local prices per good. Money-flow → goods-flow that equalises those prices across neighbours." Per settlement: `prod[g] = capability[g] × labourShare[g]`, `demand[g]`, damped scarcity `price[g]`; labour tilts `∝ price × capability` (the profit motive replaces the `_specKey` picker). Trade = per-good flow down the price gradient over the existing partner reach, bounded by surplus and affordability — "a relaxation across ticks… no world market, no iterative solver." Endowments are `grade × substantiality` (`maxRichness × (1 − e^(−S/K))`) — the RES_SCARCITY landed form; `SPEC_RELATIVE` is its mandatory complement. The ore→metal chain (`GOODS_CHAIN`), cloth quality (`GOODS_CLOTHQ` — "THE LOOMS RETURN — and are earned"), temperament demand, per-good freight (von Thünen ranges), entrepôt stocks, investment, army procurement, resource-war pressure, induced innovation, and `GOODS_UNIFY` (exportValue derived FROM the goods vector) all shipped; final income composition "history-shaped — goods 17.9 / luxuries 17.2 / pilgrims 12.1 / food 10.8 / tolls 10.1 / taxes 10.0 / mining 5.6 / materials 5.6 / capital 1.2 %." Owner decision: **grain stays on the food hierarchy** — the market carries only the tradable staple surplus.

### 1.8 Expansion / admin

Member acquisition is NOT load-priced (both attempts measured negative — §2); the working control set is: statecraft symmetry at founding/adoption, the size-target law (§1.1), `SEAT_ADMIN` (v41 — "a governed city province prices its distance at oversight, not projection"), `APPARATUS`/`APPARATUS_LOOT` (works stock funds capacity; feeds on conquest), `SATRAPIZE` (vassal integration), `PROTECTORATE` (gunboat subjugation of far coasts), `PORTER_BOUND` reach, `MARCH_FUNDED`, and the v58 tier-2 politics stack (`COURT_SPHERE`, `ENGULF_BAR=8`, `ABSORB_PEER`, `CITY_STORE`, `TRUCE_TRADE_OWN`). Amphibious war is kept, contained by comboE + WAR_REACH rather than by any bar (all multiplicative bars measured inert).

## 2. APPROACHES TRIED AND ABANDONED (with the measured reason each lost)

**Political ontology / size**

1. **Settlement-atom polities (claim bubbles / seeded Voronoi).** The original model: settlements author claims; politics moves settlements. Killed by the crux measurement: "84–89% of every realm's territory is the union of its members' worked food-catchments… Only 11–16% is capacity-driven march — the only part any capacity/FIELD_SPAN lever can move." `FIELD_SPAN=6` vs 12 was **byte-for-byte identical** — the capacity target was slack. "One entity (the settlement) carries demographic + economic + political resolution at once; the catchment radius sets all three… That is the whole case for the rewrite, now proven mechanistically" (field-polity-spec.md, THE CRUX FINDING). The naive fix (delete the stamp) "already FAILED once (smoke broke, realm count crashed, churn)"; the phase-3 field decoupling collapsed the top empire 22.3M→7.4M km² where "five rounds of capacity/reach/dominance tuning could not."
2. **The reach-Voronoi subsystem** (`SIM_REACH_BASE`, `CLAIM_POP_REF`): measured DORMANT — three compounding throttles kept every basin at the `integMin` floor; a 25× base multiplier produced "±2 tiles at step 14,000, zero before" (country-count-size-diagnosis.md finding 4). Dead code under FIELD_POLITY; "do not sweep SIM_REACH_* expecting effect."
3. **The COVER floor as the size model ("minimum Egypt").** Floored every realm to ~1.13M km² at statehood and filled it in one pass ("explode in one tick"). Floor OFF proved small realms possible but emptied the map (coverage 64%→23%) — "the median-anchored log2 means nobody pulls ahead" (empire-consolidation, MINIMUM-EGYPT FIX).
4. **SIZE_BY_POP v1 (pop cap alone):** same 32% coverage plateau — "intrinsic to the median-relative benchmark."
5. **The `world._sizePopK` global median anchor** (tiles-per-person at the median realm): with 1–8 realms in sample "the 'median' WAS the leading cohort — n=1 for a third of the reference run. One realm reaching Bronze re-sized every Stone-Age realm on the planet" ×8–11 in one window, band ×8.7–×20, coverage breathing 1.6→8.4→3.4%; also the save/load drift class ("a one-tile re-warm perturbation shifts the median"). Deleted; replaced by the per-realm memoryless density formula (design-size-target-regrounding.md; acceptance: own-state-unchanged realms move <10%/window vs ×8.76 before).
6. **The held-tiles size base** (`govPop` over owned tiles): `target = k·h`, "a pure proportional feedback with NO interior fixed point. k>1 runs away… k<1 RATCHETS… down to the one-tile anchor core… No value of the constant fixes it (a self-referential loop is bistable at every setting)" — the measured k crossing 1 mid-run was the owner-visible "slow start, then blobs of nations all over" (SIZE_WORKED desc).
7. **The flat logistics march** (`150·logi²·r2`): population-blind allowance climbing to 50% of the median target — "Every backwater with diffused roads collects a continental-power march it has nobody to administer." Replaced by MARCH_FUNDED multiple (of people-funded extent).
8. **Occupied-land / threshold-count size target:** rejected in design — circular region definition; any forager-scale threshold counts 77-100% of land so the discriminating value must sit exactly where it does outcome-fitting work; tile counts are not conserved under grid refinement where integrals are (design-size-target-regrounding §3a).
9. **Marginal-density-gated growth (candidate c)** — recorded as the future mechanism, not built (rewrites the whole target/grow/shed ledger). `MARGINAL_HOLD` exists def 0.
10. **SPAN_TECH** (earned-span multiplier on the paint): shipped 0.85 in July (THE fix for stone-age sprawl — Europe claimed 95%→33-36%), later retired at v56 with the rest of the refuted/default-0 set once the size law subsumed it (spanTechMul survives inside SIZE_BY_POP).

**Empire mortality / war**

11. **Every multiplicative war/absorb gate:** "inert because a giant governs 10–20× any neighbour and overwhelms any ratio" — `AMPHIB_BAR` at 1.8/3/5 ≈ same (only 0 helps), `ABSORB_*`, and the built-then-reverted `AMPHIB_REACH` sea-distance decay ("giants clear the bar even at 0.17× strength; the over-consolidation is from SHORT-sea hops anyway"). `CAP_DOM_MAX` inert (dominance sits below the ceiling). `AREA_LOAD` (land draws hold-capacity) inert — "the extra secession it creates is re-absorbed by the same giants" (empire-consolidation, What does NOT work).
12. **Single levers for count/size/coverage:** `FIELD_SPAN` down alone shrinks giants but "dumps land to wilderness → map empties"; `ADMIN_HALF` down or `EXPAND_RATE` up alone "make it WORSE." Hence comboE (a four-lever combination) — see §5.
13. **WAR_REACH=8** (stronger locality): rejected — "locality so strong that distance-blind PEACEFUL absorption becomes the dominant consolidator (biggest realm regressed on 4242) and death flows thin."
14. **CAP_DOM per-realm revenue dominance:** rewarded SIZE not efficiency ("total revenue is member-count × per-province take… capacity compounded on its own size"); fixed to per-province extraction vs peer per-province median.
15. **The CITY_TIER sovereignty gate:** the floating city bar pinned labelled cities to ~4 planet-wide, capping polity count and (via the dragged secession seat-bar) making empires immortal. Replaced by label-free provincial seats ("a provincial seat is the LOCALLY-STRONGEST member within a PROVINCE_SPAN… the governor's power base is his PROVINCE's aggregate power").

**Expansion budgeting**

16. **ADOPT_BUDGET (strain-gated adoption refusal):** "The gate made the cycle worse: a higher, later peak; a far deeper bust; 2.6× the polity deaths." Reasons: "Refusing subjects refuses their taxes… Freed land is a commons… Two controllers, one plant" (budget-gated-expansion.md).
17. **FISC_ADOPT (marginal-revenue adoption)** — the "corrected" mechanism: "a measured negative at every dose — the honest surprise… Monotonic dose-response, so this is the mechanism, not the tuning. Reading: in this sim's fiscal loop CAPACITY COMES FROM SUBJECTS… *any* adoption-refusal starves the mid-game hinterland absorption that funds the next ring of consolidation." Deleted v56.
18. **REACH_STRAIN:** inert (dead legacy path).
19. **FRONTIER_FOUNDING=3 dial:** more foundings → conquest fodder → fewer countries and a 40%-share hegemon at 15k, 3 soft warnings — REJECTED. (The statecraft-symmetry *mechanism* later delivered the count increase and passed 3/3.)
20. **CRADLE_ORG cut (org-only head-start reduction):** "NOT robust… the overshoot ratio is WORSE on all three seeds… The single-seed promise was trajectory chaos." Superseded same-day by full head-start removal (`CRADLE_EVE=0` — the owner chose removal; cradles seed as natural villages; the calendar epoch followed the world). ## 5. INTERFERENCE CASES (levers/mechanisms interacting badly, concretely)

1. **comboE** (empire-consolidation-2026-07.md) — the canonical case. "Single levers each fix one axis and break another; none is a silver bullet": `FIELD_SPAN` down shrinks giants but dumps shed land to wilderness (map empties); `ADMIN_HALF` down or `EXPAND_RATE` up alone make it worse ("realms collapse / the hegemon grabs the fill"); `AMPHIB_BAR` magnitudes inert. The fix was a four-lever combination whose key is that `COVER_ORG` "grows the small realms, not [the giants] — the giants are capacity-bound and sit ABOVE the floor… the key that breaks the shrink-vs-fill tension."
2. **Capacity vs the catchment stamp** — every size lever (reach, dominance, CAP_MODEL, region-capacity, FIELD_SPAN) was inert because the stamp supplied 84-89% of territory; the levers "all move the slack 15%" (field-polity-spec CRUX).
3. **Compensating errors tuned in different eras** — "'Everything shatters' was fixed by adding capacity…; 'micro-state swarm' was fixed by raising CITY_TIER. Each was validated against the complaint it addressed; nobody re-measured the PAIR, and when the secession side silently died, the capacity side had no opponent left" → immortal empires (country-count-size-diagnosis post-mortem #5). The CITY_TIER raise for *founding* silently dragged the *secession* seat-bar with it.
4. **The CAP_MODEL units error** — the review-installed replacement paid for size: "total revenue is member-count × per-province take, so a 17-province realm read ~5× 'dominant' and its capacity compounded on its own size (revenue → capacity → members → revenue)."
5. **Relief walls × ungated enclave-swallow** — adding mountain relief created pockets, and `eliminateEnclaves` (no org/capacity gate) "became the dominant consolidation flow (cradle realms at org 0.26-0.40… logging 30-41 enclave annexations each)"; mountain pockets are precisely where statelets historically survive. Fixed by giving the channel the same statecraft bars.
