## 1. NAMED PATHOLOGIES — the catalog

### 1.1 The polity mill (Egypt non-persistence)
- **Mechanism:** "The valley is a *mill*, not a state with a wound: 49 realms cycled through the box in 10k steps; the settlement register beneath them turned over 385 entities for a ~50-seat valley... 'Egypt-persistence' is really **member-persistence**: no polity of any size can outlive members with a 2–4k-step half-life." The coroner's certificates read "conquest/integrated/dissolved" but every death is downstream of seat-death, which is demographic-economic. (`egypt-autopsy-2026-08-24.md`)
- **Status:** LARGELY FIXED by the food/famine stack: LEAN_YEAR per-basin founding margins + HARVEST_YEARS (v46) — live-regime referendum: register turnover 375→93, big-anchor deaths 16→7, realms-through-box 86→20, "**one realm, 21.5k→25k unbroken**" holding the valley. Residual churn is proto-towns at the register edge.
- **Detection signature (v2 acceptance):** realms-through-a-cradle-box per 10k steps (control 49-86, fixed ~20); big-anchor deaths (peak ≥ 2M-person catchment) per 25k (control 15-19, fixed 7); register turnover per cradle (control 375-385, fixed ~93-132); one realm holding the valley for ≥3.5k steps unbroken. Note: realm-count-through-box has "huge single-seed variance (70–99 across arms); don't read deltas <2× as treatment."

### 1.2 Zero-margin packing / the overmint (the settlement conveyor)
- **Mechanism:** "the register count is pie ÷ mean-city-size... This sim's cities equilibrate at exactly 1× the minimum" — ~65 at-bar cities where bronze Egypt held 5-10, because nothing prefers agglomerating growth into an existing center over minting a new minimum-center beside it. "~6–10× history's city density on the same ground, at zero margin, which is the churn's fuel regardless of which mechanism executes the victims." Every local-availability mint bar was measured to be "a SATURATION DETECTOR — it can only prevent above-saturation minting, never cut below it." (`egypt-autopsy`)
- **Status:** FIXED at the founding side (LEAN_YEAR: found only where the basin survives its own deep year); hierarchy/differentiation half still OPEN (primacy stayed ~1.0-1.3).
- **Signature:** mean city size / minimum-viable bar (control 0.75-1.3×; fixed 2.4-4.2×); standing register vs bronze-Egypt band (~5-10 real cities per cradle).

### 1.3 The two food books (planet-wide pseudo-famine)
- **Mechanism:** the FIELD book (popField, self-feeding countryside) and the SETTLEMENT book disagreed: the ledger "bills each city demand = 0.003 × its WHOLE catchment census while crediting only its worked-tile landFood," reading production at 1/6-1/8 of demand valley-wide, permanently. Consequences: chronic fed≈0.1, empty granaries, prices pinned at max clamp, famine-vulnerability draws concentrating on "fragile" cradles, ZERO storable surplus, importShare 0.00 planet-wide, no urban hierarchy. (`egypt-autopsy`)
- **Status:** FIXED — ONE_BOOK (demand bills the market-fed core, v45) + SHIP_SURPLUS (farm gate sells surplus, world grain flow ×6). But the ledger/census divorce survives at the shipped grid in another form: "`_landFood/_coreNeed` p50 is **0.101 at the shipped grid and 8.202 at the reference grid** — an 81× regime difference in the same statistic" (`urban-claim-memo`). OPEN.
- **Signature:** importShare p90 (0.00 = dead economy; healthy worlds read 1.00 at real cities); world gross surplus vs core need; fed p50 at cities (0.27 chronic = broken); the two-book ratio ledger-food/census-food at both grids.

### 1.4 The self-sufficiency trap
- **Mechanism:** "the v46 founding law births every city locally fed → deficit ≈ 0 → the v47 deficit-only market buys nothing → no import fuel → pinned at the founding minimum with a full treasury... A world where every region exactly feeds itself has zero food trade — history broke the symmetry with DEMAND AHEAD OF NEED (the annona bought to fill warehouses)." (`harvest-years-2026-08-25.md`)
- **Status:** FIXED — GRAIN_PROVISION (v48): market need = deficit + granary refill at the pace of the city's own mouths. Referendum: peer-buyers 272/482, top3 share 0.16 ("crowding-out refuted"), Egypt fed 0.24→0.58.
- **Signature:** fraction of register buying grain (whale pattern = 3 importers; healthy >50%); top-3 import share (note: concentration itself is NOT the defect — "Rome, Constantinople and Alexandria DID dominate the ancient grain trade").

### 1.5 The grain-market leaf lockout
- **Mechanism:** "The tree market moves grain child→liege only, inside one country. A leaf — 82-84% of every register — can neither buy (no children) **nor sell** (no parent → never offers)... the world grows 2.1× its cities' needs while 84% starve. A pure distribution failure." (`harvest-years`)
- **Status:** FIXED — GRAIN_MARKET (v47, open peer market gated on coined money), with three elimination-forced rules: seed-corn (granaries refill before exports), the market-institution gate (dawn trade broke seed 777), the export-capacity add-back ("selling cannot drag a catchment below what its own land grows" — and never as a cross-tick max() ratchet, which became "a lag-discipline lesson: same-lag quantities only").
- **Signature:** importers count (3→175 obs-240); fed(leaf) p50 (0.08→0.58); LEAF share of hungry register.

### 1.6 Bloodless border arithmetic (the anchor-melt executioner)

Measured double inversion: "**sieges — the actual historical instrument of taking cities — kill nothing** (survivors sit at 75% besieged), while **bloodless border arithmetic kills a metropolis in 100 steps**." (`egypt-autopsy`)
