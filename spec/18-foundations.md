## 18.1 The primitive stack

**Level 0 — the given** (data, never simulated): terrain, monthly climate,
wild-crop ranges, domesticable animals, ores, pasture productivity, hazard
maps (volcanic, seismic, flood, storm). Real, cited, immutable (R7).

**Level 1 — conserved matter and energy** (the hard atoms): people (with
age cohorts); biomass/food in its forms (standing crops, stored grain,
herds, wood, fish stocks); built capital (worked fields, irrigation,
buildings, walls, roads, ships, tools/weapons); metal and coin. All
conserved (R5), all in real units, all directly calibrated. Derived — not
primitive — despite appearances: travel cost (terrain + climate + capital
+ capability), authority (coercion + legitimacy + edges over travel cost),
prices (bids from hunger and coin), catchments, labels of every kind.

**Level 2 — population mental states** (the one soft primitive class; we
cannot simulate minds, so populations carry belief/disposition stocks).
**The registry is closed at ten**; each holds the four-part contract —
carrier / writer (experience that changes it) / reader (behavior it
modulates) / calibration:

| Stock | Carrier | Writer | Reader | Calibration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technique proficiency | practitioner pools | practice volume | output, capability | **direct** (sequences, diffusion, Henrich) |
| Technique awareness | communities | contact | adoption hazard | direct-ish (diffusion lags) |
| Geographic knowledge | traditions | voyages, reports | trade/exploration targets | direct-ish (portolan record) |
| Identity mixes (culture/faith/language) | communities | contact, drift, assimilation | coordination, trust term, legitimacy modifiers | direct (distribution gates) |
| War assessments | belligerent pairs | battles, sieges, seasons | settlement hazard | direct-ish (war durations) |
| Legitimacy | (ruler × community) | service delivery, atrocity | enforcement cost, surrender, succession | **indirect** |
| Allegiance / homeland memory | tiles/communities | rule duration, fall events | assimilation, restoration | **indirect** |
| Grievance | communities | harms | unrest coordination | **indirect** |
| Values | cultures | practiced/rewarded life (EMA) | behavior hazards | **indirect** |
| Siege reputation | centers | honored/broken terms | surrender hazard | qualitative |

Laws of this class:
- **The latent-variable declaration**: the four attachment stocks
  (legitimacy, allegiance, grievance, values) are validated only through
  consequences (rebellion rates, restoration patterns, holding costs,
  style divergence) — like temperature before thermometers. Legitimate,
  but disciplined: every mental stock MUST be experience-written and MUST
  appear in at least one reality-gated outcome.
- **The cap**: adding an eleventh stock requires retiring one or
  extraordinary justification recorded in DECISIONS. (Reduction win
  already banked: trade "trust" needs no stock — the enforcement term
  reads identity-network presence.)
- **The bounded-feedback check**: values ↔ practice is a deliberate
  positive feedback (the lock-in); it ships with bounded weights and a
  divergence-without-runaway ensemble check.

**Level 3 — two honest others**: *obligation edges* are recorded
historical facts (the oath is in the event log) whose **live strength is
always re-derived** from the mental + physical books — an edge backed by
zero coercion and zero legitimacy decays to nothing; edges are compressed
belief-pairs plus credibility, never free-standing political truth.
*Hazard draws* stand in for sub-grid unmodeled detail, confined to the
ratified slots: individuals, founders, physical hazards — never society
dice (15c).

## 18.2 The label law

**No mechanism ever reads a derived label** — tier, era, government form,
colony type, polity name. Labels are display. Consequences flow from the
quantities the label summarizes (a coalition with no single dynastic
holder renegotiates succession — *that* is why council rule trades
succession crises for factional strife; the word "republic" does
nothing). This generalizes v1's tier-ratchet scar and the era rule; the
audit caught one violation already (17a's first phrasing — fixed).

## 18.3 Representation thresholds and the invariance gate

A distinct class of numbers that are **not physical constants**: the
community condensation bar, the center salience bar, top-K slots
(authority, identity), catalog granularities, the grid itself. These are
bookkeeping. The generalized third cardinal rule:

> **Macro-history must be statistically invariant (within chaos-twin
> bounds) under perturbation of every representation threshold.**

If doubling the community bar changes who wins the Mediterranean, the bar
is doing physics it has no right to do. This is the mechanical answer to
"unverified gesticulation": any quantity that exists only by grace of
bookkeeping granularity is exposed by wiggling the granularity. The gate
runs in 08's class A from the milestone where each threshold first exists;
the thresholds live in their own ledger section (09), separate from world
constants, each with its tested invariance band.

## 18.4 The standing audit

Every future spec change and every new mechanism answers, in review:
1. Which Level-0/1 books does it read and write? (None → what mental
   stock, and does that stock's contract cover it?)
2. Does it add a constant (ledger row), a representation threshold
   (invariance band), or a mental stock (the cap)?
3. Does anything read a label?
4. What outcome gates it against reality?
