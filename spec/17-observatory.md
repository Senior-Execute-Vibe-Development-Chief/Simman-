# 17 — The observatory  `[DESIGN DETAIL — working design per DECISIONS 14e]`

Phase 1's product (DECISIONS 4): not a game with a player, but the finest
instrument ever pointed at an emergent Earth. The shell grows with the sim
(10's standing rule — every milestone lands with its lens), and this
chapter is what it grows *into*.

## 17.1 The surfaces

- **The map** is the app: one continuous camera (12's altitude bands),
  the atlas design language carried from v1 (paper world / dark chrome),
  labels and heraldry revealed by zoom and power.
- **Lenses** — each teaches one mechanism, never drives anything (v1's
  legend-as-data doctrine): Terrain/Atlas · Politics (authority gradients
  per P4; the dominant-authority simplified lens; obligation-graph
  overlay) · Peoples/Population/Ancestry · Tongues · Faiths · Economy
  (trade, coin, goods, prices, labour, food flows) · Knowledge (technique
  frontiers, carrier density) · the **mappa mundi lens** (14 — the world
  as a chosen tradition believes it) · hazards/danger-premium overlay.
  **Emergent availability** (ported doctrine): a lens lights only when
  its phenomenon first exists — state-gated, never scheduled.
- **The codex** — the atlas's book (v1's strongest UI heritage, kept):
  living registries of realms, peoples, faiths, tongues, dynasties; every
  name a chip that navigates; realm pages with throne, arms, fisc,
  chronicle.
- **Chronicles** with the **true record / scribes' version** toggle (7.5)
  — the scribes' text filtered through the recording culture's
  perspective and its patron's legitimacy needs.
- **The event feed and epochal toasts**: a realm falls, a faith schisms, a
  first-of-its-kind discovery — click to jump the camera.
- **Time controls**: pause, speeds, and the **timeline scrubber** —
  rewind the whole world through recorded frames; 12's time-lapse
  morphology at depth.
- **The almanac** — the reality tables (08) as a player-facing surface:
  population curves, city-size distributions, empire statistics, each
  plotted against history's bands. "How history-shaped is my Earth?" is a
  screen, not a dev tool.
- **Follow-the-grain** (12.4): the signature interaction, reachable from
  any object at any zoom.

## 17.2 The sharing loop

A seed IS an alternate Earth. First-class: copy/share seed + config
identity (02's rule that config is part of world identity); **export the
world bible** (structured event log, registries, every great realm's
chronicle in both versions — v1 feature, kept); save/load full worlds;
side-by-side **seed comparison** (two almanacs, two maps, one screen) as
the concrete form of the ensemble idea. Determinism (02) is what makes
all of this possible — the same seed is the same Earth everywhere.

## 17.3 Inspection depth

Click anything, see its books: a community's food balance and legitimacy
per ruler; a center's retinue, treasury, institution proficiencies, and
obligation edges (in and out, with strength and binding); an army's
supply state; a technique's carrier map; an outbreak's path. The
observatory's promise is v1's hardest-won lesson made product: **eyes on
the map out-diagnose everything** — so every mechanism in chapters 03–16
must have a surface where a human can watch it work.

## 17.4 Checks

The watchability rule is a gate (10): a milestone without its lens does
not pass. The almanac's plots are generated from the same collector the
gates read (08.5) — one measurement pipeline, two audiences.
