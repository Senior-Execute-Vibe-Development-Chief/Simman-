# 11 — Knowledge and technology  `[FULL DETAIL]`

Ratified DECISIONS 8. Techniques as living populations: knowledge is
carried by practitioners, advanced by practice, spread by people, and
mortal. There is no civilization-level knowledge stat, no shared learning
rate, and no "science" system in Phase 1 (the tinkering economy; theory-
driven invention is Phase 2).

## 11.1 Why v1's model is rejected in kind

Six fluid "tracks" fed by one learning rate × a multiplier tower produced:
the era skew (institutions at 6–8× history's pace, metallurgy at 1.4× —
open at v1's death), flight-before-the-compass (demand bolted on late),
dark ages needing their own decay dial, and learning constants confessedly
calibrated to the display calendar — the one first-rule violation v1 knew
it had (research/02 §1.31, research/03 §4a).

## 11.2 The objects

- **Technique catalog** (data; order 100–200 Phase-1 entries; v1's tech
  DAG is the seed, re-founded): each entry has a **domain** (farming,
  smithing, building, sailing, record-keeping/administration, war, …),
  prerequisites, required inputs (ore, draft, water power…), a
  **complexity** — the minimum connected practitioner pool that can
  sustain it (the Tasmania/Henrich principle) — and effect channels.
- **Carriers**: per-community practitioner pools per domain — these ARE
  the goods economy's labor shares (06.3). No new state. Administrators
  and retinues are the carrier pools of the administration and war
  domains (DECISIONS 8b: institutions are administration-domain
  techniques; 05.5 defers here).
- **Proficiency**: per community × technique. Maintained by practice
  volume; decays without practice; hard-capped where the connected
  carrier pool < complexity (a city that loses its smiths loses smithing,
  on a lag; a palace that was the whole scribal pool takes literacy down
  with it — collapse scars are demographic, not a dial).
- **Awareness** (DECISIONS 8c): a cheap bit that diffuses easily along
  trade and contact — knowing a thing exists. Proficiency does not
  diffuse; it is rebuilt locally by practice or imported with carriers.

## 11.3 The flows

- **Improvement**: proficiency drifts up with practice volume × contact
  diversity (distinct traditions reachable) × value-at-stake (induced
  innovation — dear problems pull their solutions). No global rate.
- **Unlocks**: threshold crossings open adjacent catalog entries as
  hazard-rated recombination events, clustered where density × diversity ×
  surplus is high. Named inventors are the condensation of ripe events
  (07.4), never causes.
- **Transmission**: (1) people — craftsman migration, poaching, and
  capture, as flows on the travel field (war moves smiths; this is a
  standard war outcome); (2) goods — weak reverse-engineering awareness;
  (3) **writing, then print** — transmission without living carriers:
  each raises the sustainable-complexity ceiling and fuses distant pools
  into one effective population. Print is Phase 1's finale: loss becomes
  near-impossible, improvement compounds continent-wide, and the early-
  modern acceleration emerges.
- **Loss**: carrier collapse (plague, sack, dissolution) cuts proficiency
  with the pool; isolation below complexity sheds techniques — Tasmania
  is a required emergent outcome, not a special case.
- **Blocking** (DECISIONS 8d, full strength): where adoption destroys an
  incumbent's rents (guild revenue, elite labor arrangements, a court's
  monopoly), adoption hazard falls in proportion to the incumbent's
  authority over the adopting community. Suppression happens somewhere in
  most runs and costs the suppressor downstream.

## 11.4 Couplings

- **Era skew, cured structurally**: administration-domain proficiency
  requires practice volume — which is the actual business of ruling an
  actual economy. Record-keeping cannot sprint ahead of the material
  world it records.
- **War**: retinues carry military technique; war is its practice;
  military capital intensity (05.1 primitive 7) reads this domain.
- **Farming**: the field-level technique wave (04.1) is this system's
  farming domain expressed spatially; the wave's ratchet is the field
  view, and *forgetting* is institutional/carrier-level, exactly as v1's
  doctrine intuited.
- **Economy**: labor shares ↔ carriers; demand values ↔ improvement
  weights; goods flows ↔ awareness diffusion. One set of books.
