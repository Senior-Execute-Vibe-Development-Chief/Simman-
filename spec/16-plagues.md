# 16 — Plagues  `[DESIGN DETAIL — ratified DECISIONS 13]`

Disease pools, not disease events. Nearly every consequence lands in books
other chapters already keep.

## 16.1 Pools (13a)

Per region/community, per pathogen **class** (crowd, zoonotic, water-borne,
parasitic — the last is the static tropical belt of 04): a pool depth that
is both the standing background mortality and the population's **immunity
portfolio**. Pools grow with density × livestock proximity × connectivity
× time. Individual outbreaks are chronicle events with generated names;
the mechanics never key on named pathogens.

## 16.2 Emergence and outbreaks (13b)

- **Class emergence**: new crowd-pathogen classes appear where density ×
  livestock × time crosses threshold — the first cities breed the first
  plagues, at emergent dates (R1 by physics). Persistence requires host
  mass (measles-class needs ~¼–½ M connected hosts — a ledger row).
- **Outbreak severity = immunity gap × density**: when connectivity
  bridges populations with unequal pools, the shallow side takes the hit.
  Canonical cascades, all compositional: new eastern routes into the
  dense Mediterranean (Antonine/Justinianic shape); **the steppe corridor
  opened by an imperial peace carries the great death** (12d's danger
  premium collapse → trans-Eurasian flow → pool gap — the peace kills);
  first contact as the extreme case (14.2 — virgin-soil collapse one way,
  the malaria wall the other).
- **Endemicization**: survivors' pools absorb the class; epidemic becomes
  childhood disease; recurrences come weaker (the documented recurrence
  pattern), free.

## 16.3 Propagation and consequences (mostly pre-wired)

Outbreaks travel with people on the travel field — trade flows, armies
(siege camps as incubators, 13.3), pilgrims — at documented speeds (the
Black Death's overland crawl is a reality-table row). Consequences in
existing books: mortality debits the demographic field (R5, cohort-aware);
labor scarcity triggers the plague fork (06.4 — wages rise vs second
serfdom); carrier die-offs are institutional collapse (11 — a plague that
takes the scribes takes literacy); legitimacy shock + rebellion focal
event (05.7); **quarantine** (13c) as an administration-domain technique
emerging where trade wealth meets administrative capacity — it trades
trade income for protection, a real choice with real books.
